## 1. Pinecœne in one page

### 1.1 The human idea

A sentence, presentation, launch plan, agreement, poem, image, or business document is a projection. It places part of a richer structure into a sequence or surface for a particular purpose and audience. It can be useful and truthful while still omitting alternatives, uncertainty, provenance, dependencies, boundaries, and change history.

Pinecœne gives a **bounded, governed semantic record** a durable, inspectable form. That form can then be expressed as sober instrumentation or as art and projected differently for different recipients without silently changing what the record means.

The concise promise is:

> **A Pinecœne gives admitted relationships a form that can be understood, expressed, and carried without pretending that one projection is the whole object.**

Pinecœne can hold many kinds of work:

- a business plan;
- a product decision;
- a scientific argument;
- an agreement or promise;
- a launch plan;
- a conversation;
- a memory or relationship;
- a poem, song, or visual artwork;
- the selected history of a project;
- a Pinecœne about the making of Pinecœne itself.

The purpose may differ. The laws do not.

### 1.2 What the product is

Pinecœne is the product in which structured thought-objects are:

- received through validated packages;
- registered with durable identity;
- rendered according to their actual standing;
- inspected through exact semantic references;
- given one or more authored Expressions;
- projected through explicit Aperture grants;
- offered and encountered through recipient-safe packages;
- returned to without mutating the predecessor;
- and, after a separate authorized act, continued in a new Successor.

The long-term product surfaces are:

1. **Library** — where Pinecœnes and their versions live.
2. **Candidate Instrument** — where a pre-admission reconstruction can be inspected without being mistaken for a Fold.
3. **Fold Player** — where an admitted or fixture-authored normal form is encountered at rest and through lawful time.
4. **Anatomy** — where every visible structural feature resolves to exact permitted meaning and standing.
5. **Expression Studio** — where people create art around the structure.
6. **Projection Canvas** — where an owner defines audience, Address, Aperture, and permissions.
7. **Offering and Locket** — where an exact recipient package is prepared and consentfully encountered.
8. **Return and Successor** — where response remains immutable and a later work receives a new identity.
9. **Passport and Lineage** — where provenance, versions, limitations, hashes, and authority are visible.

### 1.3 What Pinecœne is not

Pinecœne is not:

- a raw-document Reader;
- an LLM wrapper that turns text into attractive 3D;
- an admission authority;
- a hidden ontology generator inside a renderer;
- a universal claim that thought is literally three-dimensional;
- a diagram that contains decorative evidence;
- a permission system that merely hides private material with CSS;
- a mutable file whose history is overwritten by feedback;
- the Locket alone;
- a claim of consciousness, agency, or truth created by motion or beauty.

## 2. Why Pinecœne exists

### 2.1 Documents flatten structure

Writing normally chooses one order. A relationship graph does not naturally have only one order. A document therefore compresses a partial, relational structure into a path.

That projection can occlude:

- competing interpretations;
- unresolved relations;
- rejected branches;
- changes through time;
- the authority behind a claim;
- the difference between evidence, interpretation, promise, and boundary;
- which facts a recipient was not permitted to receive.

Pinecœne does not abolish documents. It gives teams an object from which many lawful documents, views, and encounters can be cast while retaining traceable structure.

### 2.2 A projection is authored

A geometric shadow loses information. A human projection additionally chooses the lamp: angle, audience, order, tone, emphasis, omission, and disclosure.

This is why the complete system needs both products:

- Œdit examines an authored projection and proposes candidate structures.
- A named human decides what receives standing.
- Pinecœne makes the resulting governed object perceptible and projectable.

Pinecœne cannot reverse a document into one uniquely true interior. It can receive one exact admitted record and preserve what that record supports.

### 2.3 Pinecœne is a normal-form product, not an interior claim

The inaccessible lived or conceptual interior may be richer than any representation. The Pinecœne normal form is canonical only relative to:

1. an exact admitted or explicitly fixture-authored record;
2. an exact compiler and conformation profile;
3. exact standing and OPEN conditions;
4. exact permitted provenance and rights data.

It is a stable working object, not metaphysical possession of the whole idea.

### 2.4 Structure can travel without surrendering the whole

Once the record has a stable normal form, an owner can create recipient-specific projections. A board member, engineer, friend, auditor, or public visitor may encounter different lawful views of one work.

Different does not mean deceptive. Every projection declares its operator, Aperture, permitted identity, and limitations. Material outside the grant is absent from the recipient package rather than visually concealed inside it.

## 3. The complete product experience

### 3.1 Library

The Library is the durable home, not merely a gallery.

Each registered Pinecœne entry distinguishes:

- stable Pinecœne identity;
- immutable artifact version;
- record kind and standing: governed/admitted or authored fixture;
- available Structure and Expression projections;
- predecessor, fork, and Successor lineage;
- local, imported, protected, released-public, or recipient custody;
- current limitations and conformance standing.

Mutable convenience metadata—title ordering, favourites, local tags, last opened—does not alter artifact identity.

Pre-admission candidates live in a separate **Candidate Inbox/Workbench**. They have immutable candidate-object identities and hashes but no `pinecoeneId` or registered work identity. A candidate enters the Library only after a valid admitted or authored-fixture record is registered under an exact artifact identity context.

### 3.2 Candidate Instrument

The Candidate Instrument is pre-admission. It displays what a declared Reader or fixture author proposed, not what is established.

It may show:

- source-anchored candidate entities;
- competing relations;
- alternative fourth points or other structural alternatives;
- candidate edges and faces;
- pressure lines and boundary frames;
- contradictions and collapsed shapes;
- explicitly `proposed_open` material;
- provenance and candidate standing.

Candidate points appear as ghosts, scaffolds, or alternatives. Candidate edges remain incomplete. Competing candidates may coexist. No candidate receives the material treatment reserved for an admitted Fold.

The instrument never chooses the winning candidate. Selection and admission occur upstream through named human authority.

### 3.3 Fold Player

The Fold Player receives a frozen normal form or an explicitly authored fixture normal form.

It shows only:

- included entities;
- included relations;
- explicitly included OPEN conditions;
- permitted provenance;
- declared boundaries;
- exact standing;
- deterministic transitions supported by the normal form.

The settled Fold rests. Movement is used for direct manipulation, lawful transition replay, disclosure change, Locket opening, or an explicitly labelled Expression—not to imply life, intelligence, or authority.

### 3.4 Anatomy

Anatomy is the semantic inspection of the same object. Selecting a point, line, face, gap, interval, field, or transition resolves to exact semantic references.

An Anatomy entry answers:

- What is this feature?
- Which entity or relation supports it?
- What is its standing?
- Why is it present, incomplete, OPEN, or absent?
- What provenance is this viewer allowed to inspect?
- Is it semantic, interpretive, or ornamental?

Anatomy and spatial selection must resolve to the same identity. Inspection copy cannot invent meaning missing from the package.

### 3.5 Expression Studio

Expression is the creative medium around the governed structure. It may control:

- material and palette;
- photographs, textures, illustration, and typography;
- light, atmosphere, and sound;
- spatial metaphor and composition;
- camera and choreography;
- interaction and unfolding;
- recipient presentation.

The same normal form might appear as:

- a sparse Papillœn-like instrument;
- a Christmas tree carrying photographs and memories;
- a business landscape;
- a memorial;
- a miniature city;
- a musical or kinetic composition;
- a quiet technical object.

Expression can intensify understanding and feeling. It cannot alter semantic identity, entities, relations, standing, OPEN state, provenance, permission ceilings, or admission history.

### 3.6 Projection Canvas

The Projection Canvas defines how one exact Pinecœne will meet a particular audience.

It keeps separate:

- **Address** — toward whom or what the projection is oriented;
- **contact** — point, path, surface, or refused interior merger;
- **resolution** — how much structural detail is available;
- **permissions** — inspect, replay, retain, respond, download, reuse, or other explicit acts;
- **seat** — author/owner and recipient relation;
- **scene source** — Structure, Expression, or both;
- **identity disclosure** — exact, scoped, pseudonymous, or withheld;
- **time** — rest, replay, or authored performance;
- **source/provenance ceiling** — exactly which references may travel.

No axis silently opens another.

### 3.7 Offering, Locket, and Encounter

An Offering is the authorized act and exact package prepared for a recipient. The Locket is an optional consent and transport vessel. The Encounter is the recipient’s lawful experience of the projection.

The Locket is not the Pinecœne. Opening it does not grant the private normal form, ownership, or unrestricted source access.

Owner Preview and recipient Witness consume the same serialized recipient package. An owner-only binding receipt may appear beside Preview, but it never enters recipient bytes.

### 3.8 Return and Successor

A Return is a new immutable authored projection from the receiving side. It references the exact Encounter package and permitted response act.

The Return does not mutate the predecessor. Owner disposition is a separate record. If an authorized human later admits eligible Return material, the system creates a new admitted record and a new Successor identity.

```text
predecessor bytes remain unchanged
        +
immutable Return
        +
separate human disposition
        ↓
new admitted record, if authorized
        ↓
new Successor identity
```

Silence, refusal, local storage, or interface completion is never called delivery, receipt, acceptance, admission, or Successor creation.

### 3.9 Passport and Lineage

The Passport exposes what the object is and is not:

- schema and compiler versions;
- artifact and projection identities;
- hashes and asset manifests;
- record kind and standing;
- declared source coverage;
- OPEN conditions;
- conformance checks and exceptions;
- Expression authorship and rights;
- projection and Aperture identities;
- predecessor, fork, Return, and Successor relations;
- fixture, simulation, local-only, protected, released, or deferred labels.

Lineage preserves change without pretending that every earlier experiment had present authority.

## 4. Product and authority boundaries

### 4.1 Œdit / Primer

Œdit owns source-facing reconstruction work:

- exact authored-projection custody;
- source coordinates;
- Reader execution and provenance;
- candidate entities and relations;
- alternatives, contradictions, and uncertainty;
- missing-material and coverage declarations;
- candidate reconstruction packages;
- human-facing workbench support for review.

Œdit proposes. It does not silently confer standing.

### 4.2 Lakin and named human admission

Lakin names the governance boundary; it is not shorthand for autonomous model authority.

Named humans retain responsibility for:

- admitting, rewriting-and-admitting, rejecting, withholding, or leaving material OPEN;
- recognizing formative lineage separately from evidence;
- authorizing source and disclosure ceilings;
- selecting or authorizing the conformation profile when required;
- authorizing an Offering;
- dispositioning a Return;
- authorizing a fork or Successor relation;
- publication, release, acceptance, and Seal.

The system records and validates receipts for those acts. It does not perform the acts on a human’s behalf.

### 4.3 Deterministic compiler

The normal-form compiler is a Pinecœne-domain pure library in the first demonstrator, not a network service and not a Reader.

It receives:

- one validated admitted or authored-fixture record;
- one immutable `ArtifactIdentityContextV0_1` issued through proposal → any required origin authority + separate registration authority → reservation and bound to that record and compiler target;
- one exact compatible conformation profile;
- one exact compiler version.

It returns one deterministic normal form or a typed failure. It cannot:

- add an entity;
- infer an omitted relation;
- choose between candidate meanings;
- close OPEN material;
- promote standing;
- use a model;
- consult source documents;
- use ambient application state.

The library may later be published as a shared package so Œdit can preview conformance, but Pinecœne remains the authority for normal-form contract compatibility. Sharing code does not move human authority.

### 4.4 Pinecœne

Pinecœne owns:

- boundary validation;
- immutable import receipts;
- artifact registration and version identity;
- deterministic normal-form compilation for admitted/fixture records;
- candidate rendering from supplied candidate geometry proposals;
- Structure and Anatomy projections;
- Expression versions and assets;
- ProjectionOperator and Aperture compilation;
- recipient-safe packages;
- Player, Offering, Locket, and Encounter;
- immutable Return attachment;
- separately authorized Return disposition records;
- Successor compilation after a new authorized admitted record exists;
- Passport, conformance, and lineage.

Pinecœne does not own:

- source reading;
- candidate semantic inference;
- human admission;
- automatic geometry selection;
- raw private Œdit database access;
- model reasoning custody;
- publication or release authority.

### 4.5 Recipient

A recipient receives only the exact trace-linked projection and capabilities granted to that Encounter.

A recipient may, if separately granted:

- view;
- inspect permitted Anatomy;
- replay permitted transitions;
- retain or download the package;
- respond;
- reuse or remix permitted Expression material.

Receiving a package does not create ownership of the private normal form, source corpus, or admission ledger.

### 4.6 Responsibility matrix

| Act or object | Œdit / Primer | Named human / Lakin boundary | Pinecœne | Recipient |

## 5. Artifact states and state machine

### 5.1 State A — readable candidate

`ReadableCaseProjectionV0_1` is pre-admission.

It may contain candidate semantics, source-safe references, contradictions, alternatives, declared uncertainty, standing, and attributed geometry proposals. It must not contain Solid material or claim a canonical Fold.

Candidate records are immutable projections of one reconstruction state. A later Reader run or human response creates another candidate object rather than overwriting the first.

### 5.2 State B — admitted Fold

`AdmittedRecordV0_1` contains only:

- admitted entities and relations;
- explicitly admitted OPEN conditions;
- permitted provenance references;
- rights and disclosure ceilings;
- exact human authority receipts;
- declared conformation-profile authorization where needed.

After a valid identity proposal, any required origin authority, separate registration authority, registrar reservation, and context issuance, the deterministic compiler produces `PinecoeneNormalFormV0_1`. The same canonical record, identity context, conformation profile, and compiler profile produce the same canonical output, including the same normalized governed-policy projection.

An explicitly synthetic `AuthoredFixtureRecordV0_1` can use the same compiler, but fixture standing survives into every downstream projection. A fixture never impersonates observed admission.

### 5.3 State C — recipient projection

`TraceLinkedProjectionV0_1` is a recipient-safe derivative of one exact normal form under one exact `ProjectionOperatorV0_1` and `ApertureGrantV0_1`.

It contains only the permitted semantic subgraph, derived Structure/Anatomy, permitted Expression projection, assets, performance, and identity level. Hidden content is absent rather than visually concealed.

### 5.4 State transitions

```text
source projection
  → Reader run
  → ReadableCaseProjection
  → human review
  → admitted | rewrite-and-admit | reject | withhold | leave OPEN
  → AdmittedRecord
  → artifact identity registration
  → deterministic compile
  → PinecoeneNormalForm
  → Structure / Anatomy
  → optional Expression
  → ProjectionOperator + ApertureGrant
  → TraceLinkedProjection
  → EncounterPackage
  → optional ReturnProjection
  → separate ReturnDisposition
  → optional new AdmittedRecord
  → new Successor
```

Forbidden shortcuts include:

- candidate → Solid Fold without admission or fixture authorship;
- ReadableCaseProjection → recipient Offering as though admitted;
- Expression → changed semantic structure;
- hidden owner scene → client-side filtering for a recipient;
- Return → predecessor mutation;
- Return → Successor without new admission and lineage authority.

### 5.5 Absence vocabulary

The following states are never interchangeable:

| State | Meaning | Rendering rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `absent` | No material was supplied or included | Render nothing; do not infer a reason |
| `candidate` | Proposed but not admitted | Ghost/scaffold/alternative only |
| `open` | An authorized unresolved condition | Preserve an explicit gap, interval, or OPEN marker |
| `rejected` | Reviewed and refused entry | No Fold influence; owner ledger only |
| `withheld_from_record` | Intentionally excluded from the admitted record | No Fold or recipient influence |
| `admitted_private` | Part of owner normal form but restricted in disclosure | Owner Fold may use it; recipient-safe compilation removes it unless granted |
| `unknown` | Contract cannot determine state | Fail closed or render an explicit unknown state where the schema permits it |

Missing semantics must never be completed because a reference tetrahedron, sphere, cube, or artistic template happens to have a visually complete shape.

## 6. Semantic, geometry, Anatomy, Expression, and Aperture separation

The architectural invariant is:

```text
semantic data
≠ geometry proposal or conformation profile
≠ Structure/Anatomy projection
≠ Expression
≠ Aperture and recipient capability
```

### 6.1 Semantic data

Semantic data records entities, relations, OPEN items, standing, provenance references, boundaries, rights, and admitted transition basis. It contains no camera, material, animation, layout, or recipient-specific grant.

### 6.2 Candidate geometry proposal

A pre-admission candidate renderer requires geometry, but Pinecœne is forbidden to select geometry automatically. Therefore candidate geometry is an explicit, attributed proposal supplied by:

- Œdit/Primer;
- a declared Method Pack;
- a fixture author;
- or a named design/conformation seat.

Every proposal binds exact candidate semantic references and states its profile, assumptions, and standing. Multiple proposals may coexist. Pinecœne validates and renders them; it does not decide which is right.

### 6.3 Conformation profile and normal-form compilation

An admitted artifact compiles under one exact conformation profile authorized for that artifact version. The profile defines deterministic mappings from admitted semantic roles to renderer-independent structural roles.

Selection of a profile is not semantic admission and not mere decoration. It is a separately attributable conformation decision. Because the profile is part of the v0.1 normal form, changing it **requires a new immutable artifact version and new normal-form identity**. An authorized same-work continuation may preserve `pinecoeneId`; a fork or Successor receives a new work identity. The previous version is never overwritten.

This first demonstrator freezes the separation, not a universal geometry vocabulary.

### 6.4 Structure and Anatomy

Structure is renderer-neutral geometry bound to exact semantic references. Anatomy is the permitted human-readable correspondence for those same references.

Structure never invents meaning. Anatomy never expands disclosure.

### 6.5 Expression

Expression is separately versioned and separately hashed. It can introduce artistic scene objects, but those objects are typed:

| Binding | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `semantic_direct` | Direct presentation of an exact structural identity |
| `interpretive_derived` | Declared authored metaphor derived from named identities |
| `ornamental` | Art or atmosphere with no claim-bearing role |

Ornament never appears in Anatomy as evidence. An interpretive element never impersonates a direct relation.

### 6.6 Aperture

Aperture is not embedded in the canonical normal form. It is an Encounter-specific grant applied during recipient-safe compilation.

Changing Aperture changes the projection/package identity. It does not change the normal form.

## 7. Non-negotiable laws

1. **No hidden interpretation:** Pinecœne never reads raw prose to invent structure.
2. **No automatic standing:** a model, validator, compiler, renderer, or repeated agreement cannot admit material.
3. **No candidate Solid:** candidate geometry remains visibly provisional.
4. **No invented completion:** missing is rendered missing, explicit OPEN is rendered OPEN, and invalid input fails closed.
5. **No semantic mutation by Expression:** art cannot change identity, relation, standing, OPEN, provenance, or admission history.
6. **No recipient filtering after owner rendering:** recipient-safe Structure, Anatomy, performance, and assets are compiled from the permitted semantic subgraph.
7. **No private-byte leakage:** forbidden material is absent from JSON, DOM, accessibility text, geometry, animation, assets, filenames, source maps, and downloads.
8. **No mutable predecessor:** Return and disposition never rewrite the predecessor.
9. **No successor by implication:** a Successor requires a new admitted/fixture record, new identity, and exact lineage authority.
10. **No false custody language:** local simulation is not delivery, receipt, revocation, acceptance, or publication.
11. **No renderer authority:** WebGL, SVG, DOM, poster, and accessible Anatomy must express the same permitted semantic projection.
12. **No unseeded identity-bearing randomness:** deterministic placement uses exact algorithms and seeds; `Math.random()` is forbidden in identity-bearing output.
13. **No profile ambiguity:** every serialized object declares schema, compiler, conformation, canonicalization, and compatibility versions relevant to it.
14. **No silent schema upgrade:** unknown or incompatible versions fail closed; historical objects remain readable through explicit adapters.
15. **No authority laundering:** technical conformance never substitutes for human authorship, admission, Offering, Return disposition, publication, acceptance, or Seal.

## 9. Candidate JSON seam

### 9.1 Common header fields

Every wire object uses a small common set of top-level header fields. Domain fields remain discriminated and closed; there is no generic nullable `payload` bag.

```json
{
  "schemaId": "pinecoene.readable-case-projection",
  "schemaVersion": "0.1.0",
  "objectId": "rcp_demo_001",
  "createdAt": "2026-08-28T00:00:00Z",
  "producer": {
    "kind": "fixture_author",
    "producerId": "pinecoene.internal-demo"
  },
  "canonicalization": "jcs-rfc8785",
  "hashProfile": "sha256-domain-separated-v0.1",
  "contentHash": "sha256:..."
}
```

`createdAt` and transport metadata do not enter semantic identity unless a specific contract declares them semantic. Each schema declares a non-recursive hash preimage: self-hash fields are omitted, and a content-derived ID is also omitted until the digest is computed. The resulting ID/hash fields are then inserted and the complete serialized artifact may receive a separate exact-bytes hash for custody.

Standing is domain-specific and required at the exact location declared by each schema; it is not a generic header value. ### 9.5 Artifact identity proposal, reservation, and context

Work and version identity are registrar-issued inputs, not values inferred from semantic content. Two different works may have the same semantic hash.

Identity issuance is deliberately acyclic:

```text
exact record + requested origin
→ ArtifactIdentityProposalV0_1 + proposal hash
→ any required named-human origin/relationship authority binds the proposal hash
→ separate artifact-registration authority permits issuing the new work/version identity
→ registrar atomically assigns and reserves work/version IDs
→ ArtifactIdentityReservationV0_1
→ ArtifactIdentityContextV0_1
→ ordinary normal-form compilation
```

`ArtifactIdentityProposalV0_1` binds the exact record ID/hash, the exact compiler/conformation target IDs and hashes, and a closed requested-origin union. It contains no issued `pinecoeneId` or `artifactVersionId`. For a successor it also binds the predecessor, Return, attachment receipt, disposition, and requested `succeeds` relationship. `SuccessorAuthorityReceiptV0_1` authorizes that exact proposal hash; it does not point forward to an identity that has not yet been issued.

`ArtifactRegistrationAuthorityReceiptV0_1` is a separate named-human act authorizing the registrar to issue one artifact identity for the exact proposal in an exact namespace. It is never replaced by a fork, continuation, or Successor relationship receipt. One human may perform both acts, but the receipts and meanings remain distinct.

`ArtifactIdentityReservationV0_1` is created by the registrar transaction after validating the proposal, registration authority, and any required origin authority. It binds the assigned `pinecoeneId`, `artifactVersionId`, proposal hash, both authority receipt hashes where applicable, compiler/conformation target, namespace, collision check, and reservation hash. A reservation can be consumed once and cannot be silently reassigned.

### 10.4 Canonicalization

The candidate profile is:

- JCS/RFC 8785 canonical JSON;
- domain-separated SHA-256;
- full digests for governing identity;
- schema-declared sorting and set semantics;
- no timestamps, locale, viewport, device capability, network state, or random values in semantic compilation unless explicitly semantic;
- deterministic numeric normalization and geometry algorithms;
- content-addressed asset manifests.

Each contract declares its exact hash preimage. A governing digest never attempts to hash a field containing itself:

```text
canonical content preimage
  = schema-declared identity-bearing fields
    minus contentHash
    minus any content-derived objectId

contentHash
  = SHA-256(domain separator || canonical content preimage)

serializedArtifactHash
  = SHA-256(exact final serialized bytes), where exact-byte custody is required
```

Opaque registrar-issued work IDs may remain in the relevant artifact preimage when the identity law declares them inputs. Content-derived IDs follow the self-exclusion rule above. The schema and golden vector—not an implementation guess—decide which case applies.

Human-readable shortened hashes are display conveniences, never collision policy.

## 11. Candidate, Fold, and recipient rendering law

### 11.1 Candidate material grammar

Candidate rendering communicates inquiry:

- points: ghosted or scaffolded;
- alternative points: simultaneous, labelled alternatives;
- proposed edges: incomplete, dashed, or tensioned;
- proposed faces: translucent inquiry surfaces, never Solid;
- pressure: directional but non-authoritative;
- contradiction: visible collision or mutually exclusive grouping;
- `proposed_open`: visibly unresolved and explicitly candidate;
- rejected material: absent from the candidate projection unless a separate owner-review view explicitly includes the decision ledger.

Rejecting a point removes or collapses every geometry dependency that cites it. Moving a point recomputes every dependent edge, face, frame, and pressure under the exact proposal algorithm.

### 11.2 Admitted material grammar

Admitted Fold rendering communicates standing:

- only admitted and explicitly OPEN semantic material can generate structural features;
- Solid is earned only under the exact conformation law;
- boundaries generate declared edges, gates, frames, membranes, or intervals;
- OPEN remains a gap, missing face, interval, or unresolved path;
- admitted owner-private material can affect the owner view but is removed from recipient-safe projections unless granted;
- fixture material uses the same mechanics with a persistent synthetic label.

### 11.3 Recipient rendering

Recipient compilation occurs in this order:

```text
validate normal form and exact grant
→ select permitted semantic subgraph
→ regenerate safe Structure from permitted semantics
→ derive safe Anatomy from permitted references
→ project permitted transitions
→ bind only permitted Expression nodes and assets
→ create recipient identity projection
→ validate complete package
→ render
```

Filtering a fully built owner scene is forbidden because hidden counts, geometry, timings, labels, hit targets, hashes, and assets may leak.

### 11.4 Renderer neutrality

The canonical Player may use Three.js, Lit, SVG, DOM, or a poster. Every renderer consumes a role-safe projection and must preserve:

- semantic feature identity;
- standing;
- OPEN;
- selection correspondence;
- permitted Anatomy;
- declared limitations.

Pixel identity is not required. Semantic and package identity are.

### 11.5 Player boundary

The Player is stateless with respect to authority. It never reads:

- Dexie directly;
- raw Œdit output;
- a private normal form on the recipient route;
- owner admission ledgers;
- unsupported schemas;
- convenient fallback fixtures.

It receives one validated, role-safe input variant and renders it or fails closed.

## 12. Expression and art

### 12.1 Why Expression is central

Pinecœne is not only a diagram engine. The governed structure is a substrate for art. This is the major creative difference between Pinecœne and a conventional knowledge graph viewer.

A creator may place around the structure:

- feelings;
- photographs;
- memories;
- materials;
- illustration;
- sound;
- light;
- ritual;
- humour;
- seasonal or cultural imagery;
- narrative pacing.

The product must make this freedom expansive while keeping the non-mutation law mechanically testable.

### 12.2 Christmas Tree example

An admitted or fixture-authored Structure may become a Christmas tree Expression:

- structural vertices become anchor positions;
- direct semantic bindings may place particular permitted photographs at exact loci;
- interpretive bindings may turn a set of relations into a garland and disclose that metaphor;
- lights and snow may remain ornament with no semantic standing;
- an OPEN relation must remain visibly incomplete or accessible through an equivalent Structure/Anatomy path;
- changing lights, photographs, material, music, or camera changes `expressionHash`, not `semanticHash` or `normalFormHash`.

This proves both freedom and discipline. The tree is not a universal Pinecœne style. It is one Expression.

### 12.3 Papillœn-informed neutral instrument

The default technical Expression or Structure renderer may learn from Papillœn’s discipline:

- sparse exact geometry;
- restrained black, bone, and limited standing colours;
- direct selection;
- stable rest state;

Papillœn’s exact topology and domain labels do not become Pinecœne’s universal ontology.

### 12.4 Expression occlusion

Expression may visually soften or occlude only after a lawful projection has been compiled. It may not decide semantic inclusion or disclosure. If an artistic scene hides a permitted structural feature, the Encounter must provide an equivalent lawful Structure or Anatomy path and disclose the artistic suppression.

### 12.5 Assets and rights

Every asset is:

- content-addressed;
- declared in a manifest;
- attributed where required;
- attached to an exact Expression version;
- governed by explicit rights and recipient permissions;
- omitted from recipient packages when not granted.

Missing assets produce a truthful fallback. They do not change semantic structure.

## 13. Projection, Aperture, and encounter

### 13.1 ProjectionOperator versus ApertureGrant

The ProjectionOperator is authored intent. The ApertureGrant is authority. Keeping them separate prevents a desired projection from silently becoming an authorized one.

The operator may request a surface view with inspection and response. The grant may narrow it to a path view without source access or response. The effective package follows the grant and every stricter underlying ceiling.

### 13.2 Independent axes

The minimum axis set is:

| Axis | Candidate values |
| --- | --- |
| Contact | `point`, `path`, `surface`, `interior_merger_refused` |
| Resolution | `R0`–`R5` as optional named presets |
| Permission | explicit set such as `view`, `inspect`, `replay`, `retain`, `return`, `download`, `reuse` |
| Seat | typed author/owner and recipient references |
| Identity | exact, scoped, pseudonymous, withheld |
| Semantic disclosure | exact permitted entity/relation set or rule |
| Provenance | exact permitted reference classes |
| Time | rest, selected replay, full permitted replay, authored performance |
| Scene | Structure, Expression, or both |
| Assets | exact permitted manifest subset |

R0–R5 may remain friendly presets. They cannot be the authority model and cannot widen another axis.

### 13.3 Structural privacy

The privacy test is not “can the visitor see it?” It is “did any forbidden consequence reach recipient-controlled bytes?”

Excluded material must not affect:

- JSON;
- entity or feature counts;
- geometry;
- transition duration or ordering;
- collision or camera bounds;
- DOM or accessibility text;
- hit targets;
- assets or filenames;
- CSS selectors or source maps;
- downloads or exports;
- recipient-visible hashes;
- network responses.

### 13.4 Exact Preview/Witness parity

Preview and Witness use the same validated `EncounterPackageV0_1` bytes and the same Player. Preview is not a visual imitation assembled from owner state.

If owner and local/hosted recipient packages both exist for one locator and do not match exactly, acquisition fails closed. Unknown IDs never fall back to a convenient fixture.

### 13.5 Locket truth

The Locket may create anticipation, consent, and care around the encounter. It must not:

- reveal package content before the permitted overture;
- claim delivery or acceptance;
- grant capabilities by opening animation;
- become a second renderer with different semantic behaviour;
- expose owner-private identities through decoration or metadata.

## 14. Return, continuation, fork, and Successor

### 14.1 Return attachment

Return attachment creates a new immutable relationship record. It does not edit the normal form, Expression, Encounter package, or predecessor history.

### 14.2 Return disposition

Candidate dispositions include:

- propose exact returned material for a later, separate admission decision;
- narrow and resubmit for review;
- reject;
- leave OPEN;
- attach as a Muse or contextual reference where the governing vocabulary permits it;
- exclude.

Disposition records what an authorized human decided. It does not rewrite what the recipient returned.

### 14.3 Same-work continuation

A same-work continuation preserves stable work identity only when the accepted continuity law permits it. It creates a new immutable artifact version and advances an authorized head pointer without rewriting earlier versions.

### 14.4 Fork

A fork creates a new work identity that intentionally diverges from one exact predecessor version. It carries a `forksFrom` relationship and separate authority.

### 14.5 Successor

A Successor creates a new work identity in response to admitted change. It carries a `succeeds` relationship to the predecessor and binds any eligible Return and human authority.

The internal demonstrator may prove Successor mechanics with an authored fixture. That successor remains visibly synthetic and proves no real-world admission, response, or acceptance.

## 18. Technical architecture

### 18.1 Architectural principle

The UI is a consumer of compiled, validated projections. Business meaning does not live in React components, Three.js object names, CSS selectors, or route conventions.

### 18.2 Proposed package decomposition

```text
packages/contracts
  JSON Schemas · TypeScript types · discriminators · compatibility

packages/canonical
  JCS · hash profiles · typed IDs · asset identities

packages/candidate
  candidate validation · geometry dependency graph · candidate scene compiler

packages/normal-form
  admitted/fixture validator · deterministic normal-form compiler

packages/structure
  renderer-neutral Structure and Anatomy projections

packages/expression
  Expression graph · bindings · assets · lint

packages/projection
  ProjectionOperator · Aperture · safe subgraph compiler · binding receipts

packages/encounter
  EncounterPackage · Locket/Witness input · acquisition validation

packages/return
  Return · disposition · predecessor invariance · lineage

packages/repository
  immutable content store · local index · adapters · receipts

apps/web
  Library · Candidate Instrument · Player · Studio shells · Encounter UI
```

The first implementation may remain one repository and application. The package boundaries are logical and testable even if the code is not yet published as separate packages.

### 18.3 Runtime pipeline

```text
wire bytes
→ schema and hash validation
→ typed immutable object
→ pure compiler for the relevant state
→ role-safe renderer-neutral projection
→ stateless Player
```

Persistence, routes, and UI shells sit outside the pure compiler path.

### 18.4 Repository interface

The internal demo uses a new isolated local database or content store. It does not in-place upgrade the historical showcase records into governing objects.

The repository interface supports:

- put immutable object after complete validation;
- get by exact typed ID and hash;
- attach immutable relation;
- maintain non-authoritative Library indexes;
- import/export owner archive;
- import/export recipient package;
- list versions and lineage;
- fail atomically on collision, mismatch, or unsupported schema.

Legacy records remain read-only/exportable through explicit adapters.

### 18.5 Canonical Player

The Player accepts a closed union:

```text
CandidatePlayerProjectionV0_1
| CorePlayerProjectionV0_1
| ExpressionPlayerProjectionV0_1
| RecipientPlayerProjectionV0_1
```

Each variant contains only role-safe data for its state. Candidate and recipient inputs are not admitted owner inputs with flags switched off.

The producers are equally closed:

- `CandidatePlayerProjectionV0_1` is compiled only from a validated readable candidate;
- `CorePlayerProjectionV0_1` is compiled from one normal form plus its exact Structure, Anatomy, and optional Transition projections;
- `ExpressionPlayerProjectionV0_1` is compiled from the exact Structure, Expression version, and validated asset manifest, with optional safe Anatomy/Transition projections;
- `RecipientPlayerProjectionV0_1` is compiled only from fully validated `EncounterPackageV0_1` bytes and never receives an owner normal form, repository handle, or fallback fixture.

Each adapter revalidates all referenced IDs and hashes and emits one separately hashed role-safe Player projection. The Player consumes these projections; it does not join source objects itself.

### 18.6 Assets

All demo assets are local and content-addressed. No arbitrary external asset fetch occurs during rendering. A future hosted asset service requires separate rights, authorization, and delivery design.

### 18.7 Performance and accessibility

The internal demonstrator retains:

- dynamic spatial-renderer loading;
- server-visible or static hash-bound posters;
- WebGL-independent Anatomy;
- keyboard traversal and visible focus;
- reduced-motion semantic steps;
- screen-reader feature correspondence;
- responsive mobile composition;
- suspended off-screen/background loops;
- truthful fallback when storage, WebGL, or assets are unavailable.

Target performance remains 60 fps on representative desktop and at least 30 fps on representative mobile during active spatial operation. Performance never justifies removing standing or accessibility information.

### 18.8 Network and custody profile

The first demonstrator is `local_internal`:

- no analytics;
- no third-party trackers;
- no live model calls;
- no live Œdit network request;
- no accounts;
- no remote package delivery;
- no hosted private-source custody;
- no claim that `noindex` is access control.

A later protected hosted profile requires an accepted authentication, authorization, storage, logging, deletion, and release plan.

- admitted semantic changes alter downstream identities;
- rejected and withheld candidates have zero influence;
- explicit OPEN never closes;

- changing Expression changes `expressionHash` but not semantic or normal-form hashes;

- ornament cannot appear as semantic evidence;

- no-WebGL and screen-reader projections preserve standing and OPEN;

## 26. Compact team narrative

Pinecœne begins after reading has produced something structured enough to inspect.

Œdit reads an exact authored projection and proposes candidate meaning. A named human decides what may stand. A deterministic compiler turns that admitted or explicitly fixture-authored record into a stable normal form. Pinecœne makes the form visible and inspectable, lets people create art around it, and prepares exact projections for particular recipients. Those recipients receive only what the Aperture grants. Their Returns remain separate from the original. If an authorized human admits change, a new Successor is born and the predecessor remains intact.

The internal demonstrator uses synthetic JSON because the immediate goal is not to prove universal reading. It is to prove the Pinecœne instruments: candidate uncertainty, deterministic form, expressive freedom, structural privacy, immutable response, and honest lineage.

## Appendix C. Glossary

**Admitted record** — The exact semantic record a named human authority has permitted to compile, including explicit OPEN conditions.

**Anatomy** — The permitted correspondence between visible structural features and exact semantic identities, standing, provenance, and explanation.

**Aperture** — The independent grant that determines effective contact, resolution, permissions, seats, identity, disclosure, time, scene, and assets for one projection.

**Authored fixture** — An explicitly synthetic or curated record used to prove product mechanics without claiming observed human admission.

**Candidate geometry proposal** — An attributed, pre-admission mapping of candidate semantic references into proposed geometry and dependencies. It has no authority to create a Fold.

**Candidate Instrument** — The Pinecœne renderer for a supplied `ReadableCaseProjection`; it displays alternatives and uncertainty without promoting them.

**Conformation profile** — A versioned deterministic mapping law from included semantic roles to renderer-independent structural roles.

**Encounter** — The recipient’s lawful runtime experience of one exact package.

**Expression** — Separately authored art and presentation around an exact Structure. It cannot change semantic identity or standing.

**Fold** — A settled spatial projection of one exact normal form. It is not the complete inaccessible interior.

**Lakin / human admission boundary** — The governance boundary at which named humans exercise standing-bearing acts. Lakin does not mean autonomous model authority.

**Locket** — An optional consent and transport vessel for an Encounter package. It is downstream of the Pinecœne.

**Normal form** — The deterministic, renderer-independent canonical object for one exact admitted or fixture-authored record under one exact compiler/conformation profile.

**Offering** — The human authorization act that makes one exact Encounter package eligible to be presented.

**OPEN** — An explicitly authorized unresolved condition. Candidate `proposed_open` and admitted OPEN remain distinct.

**Papillœn** — A related exact graph instrument whose visual discipline can inform a neutral Structure profile without defining the universal Pinecœne topology.

**Pinecœne** — The product and durable artifact system that makes validated structured meaning perceptible, expressive, projectable, encounterable, and lineaged without taking authority over the meaning.

**ProjectionOperator** — The authored casting intent: aim, Address, selection, order, emphasis, scene, and requested encounter conditions.

**ReadableCaseProjection** — A portable pre-admission candidate reconstruction produced by Œdit/Primer or an explicit fixture author.

**Return** — An immutable authored response tied to one exact Encounter package and permission.

**Standing** — The governed state of material; it is independent from appearance, repetition, model confidence, or popularity.

**Structure** — A deterministic renderer-neutral geometric projection bound to exact semantic identities.

**Successor** — A new Pinecœne identity created from a new admitted or fixture-authored record under exact predecessor and authority lineage.

**Trace-linked projection** — A recipient-safe projection with verifiable owner-side derivation from one exact normal form and grant; it is not reversible possession of the normal form.

**Œdit / Primer** — The upstream product that reads exact authored projections and proposes candidate semantic structure with provenance and alternatives.
