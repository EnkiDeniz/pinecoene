## 7.2 The problem

Thinking leaves a trail.

Almost every artifact keeps a summary.

A newer version of the same loss arrives through fluent completion: a system may know the work so well that interpretation, additions, and closure arrive already polished.

We lack a format where:

- source and reading remain distinct;
- interpretation remains arguable;
- live work looks live;
- world contact is not confused with support or closure;
- copied appearance does not inherit authenticated standing;
- a gap is tied to a declared expectation;
- a sealed object gains a future without rewriting its past;
- expressive beauty remains free without manufacturing standing;
- formative meaning can remain visible without becoming evidence;
- contact may discover without silently admitting.

## 7.3 The compiler core

The compiler core has four load-bearing layers.

### Record

The authorized source material the owner chose to keep.

### Reading

A source-anchored, challengeable interpretation.

### Admission

The authorized seat confirms, corrects, rejects, qualifies, or leaves OPEN.

### Fold

The canonical conformation compiled from the admitted inquiry graph.

This core is not the whole Pinecœne.

# What would have to be true?

Pinecœne does not begin with the claim that thought is literally geometric.

## 8.2 Four kinds of standing

### Empirical findings

What observations or experiments have shown.

### Formal precedents

What mathematical models establish under explicit assumptions.

### Methodological precedents

How structural correspondence can be tested.

### Design rationales

Why preserving a path of inquiry may be useful.

Pinecœne’s design laws remain Pinecœne’s design laws.

A nearby citation does not turn them into scientific findings.

## 8.3 Probe list

### Similarity and geometry

Some similarity relations admit useful geometric representations under suitable constructions.

Distance may be behaviorally meaningful.

Limit:

Similarity is not always metric, symmetric, or naturally reducible to one coordinate system.

**Question opened**

```text
Which Pinecœne relations deserve a distance representation,
and which should remain typed relations?
```

### Concepts, regions, and prototypes

Conceptual-spaces research provides formal languages for:

- dimensions;
- regions;
- prototypes;
- typicality;
- salience;
- structured domains.

Convexity is useful in some contexts and insufficient in others.

**Question opened**

```text
Which structural constraints make a Fold legible
without pretending they are universal laws of mind?
```

### Abstract trajectories

Some abstract tasks show map-like or trajectory-like representational signatures.

Serious rival accounts exist.

Pinecœne does not need to settle how the brain implements abstract structure.

**Question opened**

```text
Is trajectory useful as an external representation of inquiry
even when cognition implements it differently?
```
