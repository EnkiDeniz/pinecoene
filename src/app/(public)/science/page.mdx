  # Questions the neighboring work allows us to ask.

  ## Similarity and geometry
  Some similarity relations admit useful geometric representations under suitable constructions. Distance may be behaviorally meaningful. Similarity is not always metric, symmetric, or naturally reducible to one coordinate system. [[source]](#source-shepard-1987)

  **Which Pinecœne relations deserve a distance representation, and which should remain typed relations?**

  ## Concepts, regions, and prototypes
  Conceptual-spaces research provides formal languages for dimensions, regions, prototypes, typicality, salience, and structured domains. Convexity is useful in some contexts and insufficient in others. [[source]](#source-gardenfors-2000)

  **Which structural constraints make a Fold legible without pretending they are universal laws of mind?**

  ## Abstract trajectories
  Some abstract tasks show map-like or trajectory-like representational signatures. Serious rival accounts exist. Pinecœne does not need to settle how the brain implements abstract structure. [[source]](#source-constantinescu-2016)

  **Is trajectory useful as an external representation of inquiry even when cognition implements it differently?**

  ## Alignment between meaning spaces
  Formal work makes it possible to model when distinct metric-measure spaces admit comparison under explicit conditions. Possibility is not a receipt that alignment occurred. [[source]](#source-memoli-2011)

  **Can Pinecœne distinguish candidate alignment, admitted alignment, pressure, and miss?**

  ## Structural comparison across modalities
  Representational-similarity methods allow relational structures from different systems or modalities to be compared. They do not show that Pinecœne’s structure survives translation. [[source]](#source-kriegeskorte-2008)

  **Which diagnostic invariants survive geometry, sound, story, motion, or touch?**

  ## Inquiry beyond the terminal statement
  Histories and philosophies of argument show that conjecture, challenge, counterexample, repair, warrant, and unresolved branches carry information lost by polished conclusions. They do not provide a universal event grammar. [[source]](#source-lakatos-1976)

  **What is the smallest useful Pinecœne grammar that preserves inquiry without becoming an ontology of all thought?**

  ## Measurement theory
  Measurement theory asks when one relational structure admits a faithful representation in another. That is the right genre of question. It does not prove the current compiler. [[source]](#source-krantz-1971)

  **What exact source relations, compiler invariants, admissible transformations, and decoder make Pinecœne a real representation theorem?**
