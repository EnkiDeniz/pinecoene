  # We keep the answer and discard the earning.

  Thinking has history.

  A claim may be proposed, challenged, corrected, tested, contradicted, left unresolved, returned to, or closed.

  The polished artifact usually hides most of that. The conclusion remains. The path disappears.

  > **The last sentence is not the whole work.**

<Movement id="new-loss" standing="refusal">
  <Eyebrow>THE NEW PROBLEM</Eyebrow>

  # Completion can arrive without standing.

  A fluent intelligence may already know the project, the vocabulary, the references, the style, the missing paragraph, and the probable next move.

  Its trained move is completion. Completion can be useful. Completion without standing is different.

  A live work can come back more coherent, more teachable, more elegant, and more finished while several boundaries have silently moved.

  Interpretation may arrive wearing the owner’s voice. A candidate may arrive looking admitted. Completeness may masquerade as understanding. A live record may come back sealed. New architecture may appear without a source event. Memory may be treated as authority.

  This is not only an AI problem. A person, editor, institution, committee, expert, consultant, or model can all complete a work beyond the standing they possess.

  > **Memory is not standing.**

  > **Fluency is not standing.**

  > **Reciting the rule is not compiling from the rule.**

  > **An intelligence may know the work, read the work, accompany the work, and help the work become. None of those acts gives it the owner’s standing.**
</Movement>

<Movement id="distinctions" standing="commitment">
  <Eyebrow>THREE GOVERNING DISTINCTIONS</Eyebrow>

  # The architecture is earned.

  The structure may claim only what the record, reading, and admitted standing license.

  # The interior is authored.

  Music, memory, image, voice, atmosphere, story, tenderness, humor, symbol, or silence may inhabit the earned architecture. They may illuminate the rooms. They may not move the walls.

  # The offering is addressed.

  The owner decides who may receive it, at what resolution, under what permissions, and with what right to inspect, replay, return, or decline.

  > **Expression is free. Standing is not.**
</Movement>

<Movement id="muse" standing="commitment">
  <Eyebrow>MEANING WITHOUT STANDING INFLATION</Eyebrow>

  # A Muse may matter without becoming evidence.

  A person may have changed the work. A dog may have accompanied it. A song may have opened it. A place, myth, image, memory, object, event, or synchronicity may have reoriented its becoming.

  Pinecœne calls an owner-recognized formative presence a **Muse**.

  The **Constellation** answers: **What did this form grow among?**

  A Muse may matter enormously. It does not automatically become evidence, causal proof, authorization, endorsement, co-authorship, or a recipient.

  > **Muse is how wonder enters without standing inflation.**
</Movement>

<Movement id="presence" standing="commitment">
  <Eyebrow>AI PRESENCE</Eyebrow>

  # A human earns a Pinecœne. 7 inhabits the grammar.

  A person-owned Fold is persistent, owner-held, versioned, permission-bound, and capable of Offering, Return, and Successor.

  **7 Presence** is different. It is session-scoped face, voice, sound, and observable assembly telemetry.

  7 may assist intake, propose readings, surface candidate Muses, show observable assembly state, propose candidate Quarta relations, help prepare an Offering, present a human Pinecœne within permission, and route a Return.

  7 may not inherit the Fold, inherit owner standing, acknowledge a Muse for the owner, choose Address, admit a Return, seal a Successor, or display hidden chain-of-thought.

  > **7 may present the form. 7 may not possess the form.**
</Movement>

<Movement id="kill" standing="refusal">
  <Eyebrow>THE FLOOR MUST BE ALLOWED TO DIE</Eyebrow>

  # Pinecœne must name the result that defeats it.

  If a thin record can look strongly tested or resolved, honesty is dead.

  If copied appearance cannot be distinguished from authenticated standing, standing is dead.

  If owner closure can be manufactured by style, closure is dead.

  If Expression can upgrade epistemic status, the separation is dead.

  If source or reading changes without identity changing, provenance is dead.

  If a sealed Fold silently mutates instead of gaining a Successor, lineage is dead.

  Transport and contact are allowed to die. The floor is not.

  > **If the floor dies, stop using the name.**
</Movement>
