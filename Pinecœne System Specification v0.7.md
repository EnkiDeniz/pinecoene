## 1 · Canonical product model

### 1.1 Pinecœne

A **Pinecœne** is an owner-held, versioned, renderer-independent normal form deterministically compiled from:

- exact admitted semantic material;
- admitted and explicitly OPEN relations;
- declared epistemic types and standing;
- acknowledged formative lineage;
- rights and per-part disclosure ceilings;
- a governing semantic and standing law;
- and an explicit human admission act.

The visible Fold is a spatial projection of the Pinecœne. It is not the whole canonical object.

The normal form is canonical only relative to:

1. the exact admitted record;
2. the governing semantic and standing law embodied in that record;
3. the deterministic compiler profile and version.

It is not:

- the inaccessible private interior of a person or organization;
- a claim that one true geometry was hidden in a document;
- the complete mathematical solution set of a source;
- a Reader output;
- a Three.js scene;
- an Expression Profile;
- an Offering package;
- a transport or acceptance receipt;
- a claim of external truth, publication, release, or Seal.

### 1.2 Projection family

The principal projections are:

| Projection | Function |
|---|---|
| **Document** | sequential or surface-authored view |
| **Glyph** | near-point or low-resolution view |
| **Şekli / Fold view** | settled spatial view |
| **Trail / Becoming** | temporal and causal view |
| **Semantic anatomy** | accessible typed inspection view |
| **Lineage** | provenance and standing view |
| **Passport / Conformance** | identity, law, evidence, and exception view |
| **Offering** | recipient-safe governed projection |
| **Locket** | consent and transport vessel for an Offering |
| **Return** | new authored projection from the receiving side |

No single projection is the complete interior or the canonical thought.

### 1.3 Two authoring paths

#### Ordinary authoring

Most historical documents are authored directly from a situated interior. No Pinecœne is presumed to have existed before them:

```text
situated interior → ordinary authored projection
```

#### Governed generation

Once a Pinecœne exists, an owner may create a trace-linked projection under declared casting conditions:

```text
Pinecœne normal form → projection operator → aperture grant → trace-linked projection
```

The two paths must never be conflated. An ordinary document cannot claim a trace link it does not possess.

### 1.4 Human acts

The owner or authorized human seat performs distinct acts:

1. **Admission** — decides what enters the admitted record.
2. **Recognition** — acknowledges formative lineage separately from evidence.
3. **Projection authorship** — selects angle, aim, seats, order, emphasis, and occlusion.
4. **Expression** — chooses lawful material and performance treatment.
5. **Address** — points the projection toward a recipient or field.
6. **Aperture authorization** — grants effective contact, resolution, and permissions within all ceilings.
7. **Offering authorization** — authorizes the exact recipient-safe package.

No model, Reader, compiler, renderer, carrier, recipient silence, or repeated agreement substitutes for these acts.

## 3 · Foundational distinctions

V0.6 distinctions remain normative. V0.7 adds:

### 3.1 Ordinary projection

A situated authored plate without a required prior Fold or verifiable normal-form lineage.

### 3.2 Governed projection

A projection generated from an admitted Pinecœne under a declared operator and grant.

### 3.3 Reader run

One exact provisional reconstruction act under declared:

- source set;
- model or human Reader identity;
- isolated role;
- Method Pack or method version;
- task contract;
- prompt hash where applicable;
- tool and source access;
- run configuration;
- communication and dependency history.

Reader provenance may alter candidate proposals. It does not enter the deterministic Fold compiler.

### 3.4 Candidate reconstruction set

The explored alternative structures returned by one or more Reader runs. It is not proof that every lawful reconstruction has been found.

### 3.5 Admitted record

The exact semantic entities, relations, standing, acknowledged lineage, evidence references, rights constraints, and item-level OPEN conditions authorized to compile.

Rejected, withheld-from-record, pending, and unreviewed candidate payloads are not part of the admitted semantic record. An admitted semantic item may still have an owner-private disclosure class and a restrictive disclosure ceiling.

### 3.6 Pinecœne normal form

The deterministic renderer-independent canonical object for an exact admitted record and compiler profile.

### 3.7 Projection operator

The declared casting conditions:

- angle;
- aim;
- author and recipient seats;
- requested contact dimension;
- requested disclosure resolution;
- requested permission vector;
- ordering;
- emphasis;
- occlusion and exclusion;
- projection grammar version.

### 3.8 Trace link

Verifiable lineage to a normal form and its casting conditions. A trace link is not mathematical invertibility, possession, permission, or access.

### 3.9 Reconstruction residual

The typed difference between an admitted intended state and admitted observed or as-built evidence.

### 3.10 Conformance

Evidence that a declared subject satisfies declared checks under a versioned profile. It does not prove truth, completeness, independence, authorship, rights, acceptance, publication, release, or Seal.

### 3.11 Two meanings of compile

The product must distinguish:

- `evaluateProjectionRealizability(document, envelope)` — a provisional inverse-reading operation that returns a typed report;
- `compileNormalForm(admittedRecord, compiler)` — a deterministic product operation.

`ProjectionRealizabilityReportV0_1` separates:

```text
internalResult = realizable | realizable_with_open | unrealizable | undetermined
worldResult    = compatible | incompatible | not_tested | inaccessible | open
```

Its findings are source- and law-linked and may include:

```text
internal_unrealizable
world_incompatible
unsupported_mark
projection_object_confusion
open_condition
inaccessible_evidence
```

An internally realizable document may still be world-incompatible. A world-facing observation cannot prove that one unique inaccessible interior produced the document. `undetermined`, `not_tested`, `inaccessible`, and `open` must not be collapsed into `false`.

## 4 · Standing, epistemic type, and admission

Standing and epistemic type are orthogonal.

### 4.1 Candidate epistemic types

```text
source_assertion
observed_evidence
declared_relation
derived_theorem
embedding_condition
authored_reading
authored_operator
planned_commitment
implementation_evidence
human_testimony
boundary_or_refusal
unsupported_addition
open_question
formative_lineage
projection_instruction
```

An item may be, for example:

- a `derived_theorem` at `candidate` standing;
- an `authored_operator` at `admitted` standing;
- `implementation_evidence` whose external coverage remains `OPEN`;
- `human_testimony` admitted as testimony without becoming executable proof.

### 4.2 Terminal dispositions

Every candidate receives exactly one disposition before record freeze:

```text
admit
rewrite_admit
leave_open
reject
withhold_from_record
```

`leave_open` is terminal but unresolved. It is not pending.

`withhold_from_record` means the candidate remains in authorized owner custody but does not enter the admitted record or affect any compiled output. It is distinct from an admitted semantic item whose disclosure class is `owner_private`, `protected`, `sanitized_public`, or `public`.

### 4.3 OPEN law

OPEN exists at item and relation level inside an admitted record.

OPEN may earn:

- a gap;
- missing face;
- unresolved path;
- refusal;
- interval;
- cool open light;
- explicit textual uncertainty.

OPEN may not be coerced into Solid, closure, omission, rejection, or failure.

### 4.4 Exclusion law

Rejected, withheld-from-record, pending, and unreviewed material may remain in owner custody and private Lineage. It must have zero effect on:

- admitted-record semantic identity;
- Fold identity;
- topology;
- scene;
- motion or audio;
- semantic anatomy;
- DOM or accessibility text;
- recipient package;
- download;
- screenshot produced from the recipient projection;
- public conformance payload;
- recipient network response.

### 4.5 Admission trace

Every standing-bearing normal-form feature cites:

- an included semantic entity or relation;
- an included admission-decision reference;
- source or evidence references where permitted;
- its epistemic type;
- its current standing;
- applicable invariant or OPEN condition.

The compiler receives only the admitted record. It must not receive Candidate Sets, Reader Runs, rejected or withheld-from-record payloads, or the complete Admission Ledger.

### 5.2 Feature classes

Rendered features are typed:

```text
semantic   carries standing-bearing normal-form meaning
expression supports lawful presentation without changing standing
interface  operates or explains the instrument
```

Only semantic features may imply evidentiary or structural standing.

### 5.3 Earned materiality

V0.6 Solid, Particulate, Edge, Quarta, OPEN, and Charge remain. V0.7 adds:

- structural topology is produced only from admitted entities and relations;

### 5.4 Rest-first law

The settled Fold rests.

Motion is lawful for:

- direct visitor operation;
- causal replay;
- disclosure transition;
- Locket opening;
- observed lifecycle transition;
- an explicitly disclosed Expression.

Ambient motion may not imply life, consciousness, intelligence, importance, or standing.

### 5.5 Visual versus contact dimension

A visually volumetric Fold does not imply 3D merger of interiors. Visual dimension and topological contact dimension are different variables.

Papillœn's 0D/1D/2D/3D-refusal grammar remains a candidate aperture profile, not a universal prohibition on Pinecœne's spatial renderer.

## 6 · Projection and Aperture

### 6.1 Independent axes

Every governed projection declares:

```text
d = requested and effective topological contact dimension
r = requested and effective disclosure resolution R0–R5
p = requested and effective permission vector
s = author and recipient seat relation
```

The candidate contact vocabulary is:

```text
d0_point            = point contact
d1_path             = path contact
d2_surface          = surface contact
d3_interior_merger  = requested interior merger
```

Under the current candidate Fold/Offering profile, `d3_interior_merger` is refused and produces a refusal trace; it never produces interior bytes. Spatial Fold, temporal Becoming, document, anatomy, and Offering are projection or performance kinds, not values of `d`.

No axis silently opens another.

Increasing resolution must not automatically:

- widen permission;
- change seat;
- widen contact;
- confer ownership;
- grant access to the normal form.

Changing permission must not silently change semantic resolution or contact.

### 6.2 Resolution

The current resolution ladder remains:

```text
R0 Mark
R1 Card / bounded record
R2 Form
R3 Inspect
R4 Replay
R5 Recital / eligible Return surface where separately granted
```

Resolution expresses available projection structure. It is not a permission bundle.

### 6.3 Permission vector

Candidate permissions include:

```text
view
inspect
replay
retain
compare
return
share
download
```

Expiry, withdrawal, retention, onward transfer, and recipient controls remain separate qualified conditions. The carrier must not promise enforcement it does not possess.

This vocabulary is canonical for v0.7. Quotation and reuse remain explicit rights conditions to be modeled in a later permission amendment; they are not silently inferred from `retain`, `share`, or `download`.

### 6.4 Aperture grant

The effective projection is the lawful intersection of:

- normal-form per-part ceilings;
- source visibility and rights constraints;
- Address and seat relation;
- requested contact dimension;
- requested resolution;
- requested permissions;
- purpose;
- recipient accessibility requirements;
- Offering authorization.

Denied dimensions and permissions remain explicit with reasons.

### 6.5 Minimum sufficient aperture

Candidate guidance:

> **Use the smallest lawful contact, resolution, and permission configuration sufficient for the declared encounter.**

This principle remains candidate and must be tested rather than treated as a universal optimum.

## 8 · Reader, tomography, and reconstruction residual

### 8.1 Reader/compiler separation

Reader model, human role, Method Pack, prompt, source/tool access, and run configuration belong to Reader provenance.

Required invariance:

```text
same admittedRecordSemanticHash
+ same deterministic compiler profile/version
= same foldId and normal-form semantic bytes
```

Changing the Reader may change `readRunId` and candidate provenance. It may not change the Fold when the admitted record is byte-identical.

### 8.2 Beam provenance

Each reconstruction beam records:

- source;
- access;
- method;
- incentive;
- time;
- role;
- angle;
- evidence base;
- Reader configuration;
- known dependencies;
- correlation risks.

Independence is a typed human assessment with rationale:

```text
independent
partially_correlated
correlated
unknown
```

It is not an invented confidence score.

### 8.3 Tomographic law

Multiple beams must be preserved before intersection. Contradiction is surfaced, not averaged away. Duplicating or paraphrasing a correlated beam does not create new independent evidence.

### 8.4 Intended and as-built states

Where actual evidence supports them, a reconstruction case may retain:

```text
P0 · intended admitted assembly
P1 · as-built admitted evidence assembly
Δ  · typed residual
```

P1 never rewrites P0. A later Return or observation creates candidate material for another admitted state or Successor.

### 8.5 Residual types

Residual items are typed individually:

```text

altered
omitted
unsupported
inaccessible
```

Each residual names:

- intended source reference;

- epistemic type;
- comparison-law version;

- owner disposition;

No single percentage may conceal OPEN, inaccessible, or unsupported dimensions.

### 14.1 Reader/compiler invariance

### 14.3 OPEN

- pending items prevent record freeze;
- item-level OPEN compiles as explicit gap, refusal, or unresolved path;
- OPEN never creates closure or Solid;
- OPEN-to-admit changes admitted record and Fold identity;
- inaccessible evidence remains inaccessible or OPEN, never convenient failure or omission.

### 14.4 Renderer neutrality

- changing Expression, camera, viewport, renderer, quality tier, reduced-motion view, or fallback does not change Fold identity;
- Address changes projection orientation only;
- Expression changes no semantic topology or standing;
- every semantic feature traces to included admission and source/evidence references.

### 14.5 Projection axes

- pairwise tests cover contact, R0–R5, permissions, and seats;
- higher resolution does not escalate permission or contact;
- wider permission does not change resolution;
- a new recipient seat creates a new operator/projection;
- requested 3D merger under a refusing profile produces a refusal trace and no interior bytes;
- Preview and Witness consume exact package bytes.

### 14.6 Identity and trace

- golden JCS/SHA-256 vectors reproduce across supported environments;
- corrupt, mismatched, unknown, and stale identities fail closed;
- trace links verify to declared scoped commitments without leaking private storage identifiers;
- ordinary projections never claim governed lineage.

### 14.7 Reconstruction and tomography

- beams are preserved independently before intersection;
- dependence and correlation risks are disclosed;
- duplicate/correlated beams do not increase independence standing;
- contradictions remain visible;
- residuals are typed and attributable;
- residuals and Returns do not mutate an existing Fold without admission.

### 14.8 Projection realizability

- internal realizability and world compatibility are evaluated separately;
- every finding cites source coordinates, applicable law, evidence, and standing;
- unsupported marks and projection/object confusion cannot compile as admitted structure without a human decision;
- `undetermined`, `not_tested`, `inaccessible`, and `open` never collapse into `false` or `unrealizable`;
- identical inputs and realizability profile produce an identical report identity.

### 14.9 Public and lifecycle truth

- conformance never promotes truth, acceptance, release, or Seal;
- a local Return is never called delivered, received, or accepted;
- a Return remains immutable after disposition;
- fixture/local Returns cannot form a Successor;
