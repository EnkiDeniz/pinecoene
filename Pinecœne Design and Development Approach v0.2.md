# Pinecœne Design and Development Approach

**Version:** 0.2

**Date:** 2026-08-26

**Status:** Candidate working approach for Showcase V0 and its upgradeable product kernel

**Inputs:** Pinecœne System Specification v0.6, Concept 1 audit, Concept 2 audit, supplied visual concepts, and the reviewed future Œdit integration boundary

> **Candidate plan only.** This document authorizes no runtime, model call, source processing, account creation, deployment, release, Seal, or claim of human acceptance. Intended behavior is not evidence that the behavior exists.

**Implementation truth at 2026-08-26:** this repository contains specifications, concepts, and audits but no committed application baseline or verified hosted Pinecœne product. Every capability below remains `specified` until separately implemented and evidenced.

## 1 · Decision

Build one production-directed Pinecœne object and player inside a deliberately bounded public showcase.

The showcase has two paths:

1. **Flagship Encounter** — D. offers “I have been thinking about you.” A visitor receives the closed PCN-0002 Locket, opens it, witnesses its admitted fixture history become form, and inspects one unresolved seam.
2. **Make Your Own** — a visitor enters a short exact record, authorizes one bounded proposal read, disposes every event and relation candidate, separately acknowledges or rejects every Muse and Muse relation, chooses a bounded Expression, and opens or exports the resulting browser-local Pinecœne.

The Flagship proves desire, material identity, causal opening, inspectability, disclosure, and recipient clarity. Make Your Own proves that the same protocol, engine, and player can produce another object without pretending the complete Atelier or Œdit reading system already exists.

The first release does not claim authenticated ownership, verified recipient identity, remote delivery, cross-device Return, enforceable expiry or revocation, a completed human Œdit, arbitrary-document understanding, universal thought mapping, or the complete Studio.

Concept 2 remains an interaction reference. It is not the final visual reference.

Throughout this approach, **Showcase Conformance Profile** means the bounded contract and verification subset of the Fold Profile. It is an implementation/evidence boundary, not a third Pinecœne product profile.

> **One beautiful, truthful Pinecœne; then the instrument that lets people make more.**

## 2 · Showcase truth boundary

| Standing | Included in Showcase V0 |
|---|---|
| **Real** | Versioned source, processing authorization, candidate, admission, score, disclosure, right-to-offer authorization, performance, evidence, private-owner-archive, and recipient-package contracts; exact source hashing and anchors; complete human disposition; deterministic Fold compilation; disclosure before rendering; one complete serialized `OfferingPackage` used by Preview and Witness; real Three.js and semantic fallback renderers; keyboard, reduced-motion, sound-off, and mobile behavior; browser-local saving; distinct private and recipient-safe JSON formats; verified hashes under the declared Showcase Conformance Profile. |
| **Simplified** | PCN-0002 uses a human-curated fixture reading; Genesis Reader V0.1 accepts only short text or Markdown and proposes a small bounded event set; rights and identities are owner declarations, not verified facts; Return is same-device; the Expression palette and source formats are narrow. |
| **Fixture-authored** | Flagship sender, recipient, dedication, admission, Muses, opening, right-to-offer authorization, consent-review, and Return states are specimen data. The semantic map is authored and frozen rather than discovered live. |
| **Local-only** | Make Your Own records, scores, Offerings, and demonstration Returns remain in the current browser and device unless explicitly exported. A local `/w/:id` route is Preview, not delivery. |
| **Deferred** | Accounts, cloud synchronization, authenticated ownership, capability delivery, hosted relay, remote Return, enforceable expiry or revocation, signatures, network-free `.pcn.html`, complete Atelier, successor management, 7 Presence, and live Œdit integration. |

The flagship must carry this controlling disclosure:

> **This specimen is curated fixture content. Its sender, recipient, admission, opening, consent, and Return states do not establish that those human acts occurred, that delivery happened, or that an external truth was verified.**

The interface must say **Saved in this browser**, **Open local preview**, **Private owner backup — do not share**, **Recipient-safe Pinecœne**, and **Local demonstration Return** where applicable. It must not say sent, delivered, received, verified, revoked, synchronized, released, or sealed unless those claims have been implemented and separately evidenced.

A Showcase Conformance Profile hash proves content integrity under that profile. It does not prove identity, authorship, rights, legal consent, external truth, human acceptance, release, or Seal.

The implementation-status axis is `specified`, `fixture_authored`, `prototype_only`, `implemented_local`, `implemented_hosted`, or `verified`. Human acceptance, release, and Seal are separate evidence axes with named authority and proof. None implies another, and none is embedded in the semantic score hash.

## 3 · Non-negotiable qualities

The work must satisfy all of these at the same time:

1. **First-sight wonder** — the closed Locket is specific, ownable, materially convincing, and unmistakably Pinecœne.
2. **Causal legibility** — every meaningful visual, sonic, and textual feature can explain why it exists.
3. **Human clarity** — visitors receive value before learning the complete ontology.
4. **Source/read/standing separation** — exact source, machine proposal, owner decision, immutable origin standing, mutable real-world evidence, and external truth remain distinct.
5. **Human admission** — every event and Fold relation is kept, rewritten and kept, left OPEN, or removed; every Muse and Muse relation is separately acknowledged or rejected before compilation.
6. **Authority integrity** — model proposals, Muse recognition, recipient consent, Address, Return reuse, and owner disposition remain separate acts.
7. **Projection truth** — Preview and Witness playback consume the same hash-consistent serialized Offering package through the same player.
8. **Stillness** — motion happens because an event happens; settled objects do not perform decorative life.
9. **Accessibility parity** — reduced motion, sound off, keyboard, semantic text, high contrast, touch, zoom, and no-WebGL paths preserve permitted meaning.
10. **Portability path** — private `*.pcn.owner.json` round-trips owner custody; recipient `*.pcn.offer.json` contains no private state; network-free `.pcn.html` remains a later acceptance gate.
11. **Standalone creation** — an Œdit account is never required to make or encounter a Pinecœne.
12. **Responsive dignity** — Flagship, maker, Studio, and Preview remain coherent on mobile and desktop.
13. **Transport honesty** — local, hosted, downloaded, delivered, expired, revoked, and received are used only when their exact behavior is implemented.
14. **Upgradeability** — fixture, lightweight, and manual readers terminate at one candidate contract; the future Œdit envelope adapter emits that same candidate contract plus an import receipt; the engine and player depend on neither reader nor Œdit internals.

## 4 · The three bounded experiential worlds

### 4.1 Flagship Locket

The public flagship should feel like receiving a private object or letter:

- almost no interface chrome;
- sender, dedication, and origin disclosure before system terminology;
- a materially credible closed object;
- Open, Not now, and Decline without pressure;
- one causal opening score;
- direct inspection of Solid, Return, Muse, Edge, and OPEN;
- explicit fixture standing;
- no account wall, owner controls, or fake delivery state.

### 4.2 Make Your Own

The maker is a calm sequence, not a miniature professional Studio:

1. **Your record** — enter no more than 16 KiB of plain text or Markdown and declare rights and shared-material category.
2. **Possible meaning** — authorize one declared Genesis Reader V0.1 request and inspect no more than seven source-linked event proposals, or use the manual provider.
3. **What enters** — dispose every event and Fold-relation candidate; acknowledge or reject every Muse and Muse-relation candidate separately.
4. **Give it form** — choose dedication, Address label, bounded material and motion expression, then Preview through the actual Witness Player and save or export locally.

No automatic reader may confer owner standing. No inferred emotion, hidden influence, personality claim, unsupported quotation, or external-fact claim belongs in this path.

### 4.3 Future Atelier

The future owner environment remains a conservation table or careful atelier:

- the Pinecœne remains the primary object;
- one human decision is foregrounded at a time;
- advanced grammar is inspectable rather than constantly dominant;
- exact recipient Preview temporarily takes over the stage;
- authority changes are explicit;
- desktop supports depth and comparison;
- mobile becomes focused tasks rather than a compressed console.

The complete Atelier, recipient delivery, Return inbox, successor workflow, and Œdit bridge are target language only and are outside Showcase V0 launch.

## 5 · Design phase

Design is accepted only when the object, interaction, responsive behavior, accessibility alternatives, origin disclosure, and Flagship/maker boundary have been resolved. A polished homepage or attractive still is insufficient.

### D0 · Freeze the flagship meaning

Freeze one semantic PCN-0002 fixture through expected player input:

```text
exact fixture source
  → curated candidate reading
  → fixture-authored admission record
  → PinecoeneScore
  → DisclosureProjection
  → PerformanceScore
  → OfferingPackage
  → Witness Player
```

The fixture contains:

- sender D.;
- dedication “I have been thinking about you.”;
- seven phases and six finished phases;
- one deliberately unpraised gap;
- at least three acknowledged Muses and one private Muse;
- sparse Return particulate;
- one final OPEN seam;
- one local demonstration Return;
- successor/Muse reuse set to not permitted in the local demonstration;
- same-browser deletion available and explicitly not described as remote withdrawal.

Freeze the exact fixture text, curated candidates, fixture-labeled decisions, admitted score meaning, causal map, intended visible/private disclosure, expected cue sequence, recipient-safe origin disclosure, and private-field absence assertions. Record stable human-readable IDs, but do not claim golden hashes before canonical framing exists. Every art study uses this exact meaning.

**Gate D0:** the semantic fixture, decisions, standing, expected disclosure, cue intent, and private-field absence are agreed and frozen as protocol inputs and expected outputs. Golden framed bytes and hashes are an E1 deliverable.

### D1 · Select the flagship art direction

Compare exactly three materially distinct studies of the same Locket in the same meaningful states:

1. **Luminous archival fold** — vellum, fiber, blind embossing, warm metal joinery, raking museum light.
2. **Mineral reliquary** — translucent stone laminations, inclusions as Returns, a fault-line OPEN seam, mass, and quiet shadow.
3. **Dark instrument** — smoked or oxidized metal planes, engraved provenance, a narrow living seam, and restrained mechanical unfolding.

Each direction shows desktop and mobile arrival, closed detail, mid-opening, settled form, seam inspection, reduced-motion stills, and sound-off treatment. Layout, copy, camera intent, and fixture meaning remain constant.

**Gate D1:** select one direction or one explicitly defined hybrid based on specificity, material credibility, causal readability, emotional tone, mobile strength, accessibility, and technical feasibility.

E0 foundation work and E1 protocol implementation may begin after D0 without waiting for material selection. Flagship material rendering begins only after D1 locks the art direction. D2–D4 may then proceed in parallel around the frozen fixture and contracts.

### D2 · Define the minimum reusable object grammar

Define:

- closed silhouette and scale rules;
- semantic geometry for Solid, Return particulate, Muse particulate, Edge, Address, and OPEN;
- material library and lawful variation limits;
- lighting, camera, composition, and quality tiers;
- event-scored motion and material sound vocabulary;
- closed, opening, settled, focused, OPEN seam, and Return states;
- mobile and desktop composition;
- semantic anatomy, poster, reduced-motion, sound-off, and no-WebGL forms;
- typography, editorial voice, origin badge, and standing drawer.

**Gate D2:** a second designer or developer can reconstruct the object and every declared state without inventing semantic or authority rules.

### D3 · Prototype the Flagship Encounter

Prototype:

1. arrival and fixture disclosure;
2. Open, Not now, and Decline;
3. causal opening score;
4. settled inspection;
5. exact authored-basis explanation;
6. local demonstration Return;
7. quiet local confirmation.

Test desktop pointer, mobile touch, keyboard, reduced motion, sound off, semantic text, 200% zoom, and one low-performance profile.

**Gate D3:** a new visitor understands what is real, what is fixture-authored, why the seam remains OPEN, and what a local Return does without coaching.

### D4 · Design Make Your Own

Design the four focused steps defined in §4.2 with:

- exact source and provider authorization copy;
- no more than seven event cards, ten Fold-relation cards, three OPEN event candidates, three Muse cards, and three Muse-relation cards;
- exact quotation, proposed reading, uncertainty, and owner decision on every event and relation card;
- separate Muse and Muse-relation recognition;
- explicit immutable-origin-standing summary and current evidence summary before compilation;
- exact selected permissions and a separate right-to-offer confirmation bound to the full recipient-visible intent and Address;
- a recipient-safe Resolution 3 Inspect projection that keeps source excerpt, owner reading, and standing distinct without affecting topology;
- bounded expression controls that cannot alter topology;
- authorization and compilation before actual-player Preview of the exact package bytes;
- save/export activation of those already-previewed bytes, with any change returning to authorization and Preview;
- local save and restore, clearly separated private-backup and recipient-safe downloads, and clearing-storage warning.

**Gate D4:** a first-time owner can make a second Pinecœne without believing the reader is Œdit, the machine established truth, or the local Preview was delivered to another person.

### D5 · Showcase acceptance and handoff

Produce:

- selected art direction and object grammar;
- flagship and maker responsive flows;
- state atlas and motion score;
- source/read/standing copy deck;
- local-only and unavailable-capability copy ledger;
- accessibility alternatives;
- design tokens and component inventory;
- asset plan and performance risks;
- implementation acceptance checklist.

**Design acceptance gate:** Flagship and Make Your Own use the same protocol, engine, disclosure, and player; simplified and deferred behavior is visible; implementation has a selected material target.

## 6 · Development architecture

### 6.1 Technology choices

- **Application:** strict TypeScript, React, Next.js App Router, and Vercel.
- **Workspace:** pnpm, ESM-first packages, and pinned stable versions at bootstrap.
- **Controls:** React Aria primitives with Pinecœne-owned styling and tokens.
- **Protocol:** JSON Schema 2020-12, Ajv, generated/shared TypeScript types, the v0.1 framed RFC 8785 JCS object, and SHA-256 through Web Crypto.
- **Engine:** pure framework-neutral TypeScript for human-admission projection, Fold compilation, disclosure, and performance score.
- **Player:** Lit Web Component so the same Witness Player can run in Next.js, a future Œdit surface, or a standalone package.
- **Visual renderer:** direct Three.js/WebGL2 behind renderer-neutral scene contracts; React may host but does not own semantic rendering.
- **Fallback:** semantic DOM anatomy and canonical poster generated from the same Offering package and its semantic-text/reduced-motion tracks.
- **Audio:** native Web Audio with deterministic schedules and user-triggered start.
- **Reader boundary:** one stateless Next.js Route Handler on Vercel with a replaceable server-only structured-output model adapter, explicit request authorization, redacted operational logs, and no application persistence.
- **Custody:** IndexedDB through a `CustodyStore` adapter; Dexie may implement the first adapter.
- **Verification:** Vitest, Playwright, contract vectors, browser evidence, accessibility paths, performance budgets, and disclosure-leak tests.

Do not add WebGPU, WASM, a server database, shared auth, relay service, `.pcn` archive, or workers in Showcase V0. The one stateless Genesis Lite Route Handler is a bounded processing trust zone, not custody. Add a Worker only after measurement shows the main-thread budget is not met.

### 6.2 Small physical workspace

```text
apps/
  web/          public entry, flagship, maker, local Studio, Witness Preview, stateless Genesis route, dev-only renderer lab

packages/
  protocol/     schemas, types, canonical framing, hashes, reading-provider contracts
  engine/       admission, Fold compilation, disclosure, performance score
  player/       Lit Witness Player, Three.js, DOM/poster fallback, audio
  fixtures/     frozen PCN-0002, adversarial, browser, and accessibility fixtures
```

Rules:

- `protocol`, `engine`, and `player` remain framework-neutral;
- the renderer lab is a development-only route, not a second product;
- Three.js and fallback renderers remain player modules until an independent consumer requires separation;
- Genesis Lite provider implementation belongs behind the reading interface, not inside the engine;
- IndexedDB remains behind `CustodyStore`;
- canonical JSON owner/recipient formats live in `protocol`; self-contained HTML/archive building remains an app script until `.pcn.html` becomes real;
- additional packages require an observed dependency boundary.

### 6.3 Reading and Œdit upgrade seam

```ts
type ReadingRequestV0_1 =
  | {
      kind: "external_processing";
      source: SourceEnvelopeV0_1;
      authorization: SourceProcessingAuthorizationV0_1;
    }
  | {
      kind: "local_no_external_processing";
      source: SourceEnvelopeV0_1;
      reason: "fixture" | "manual";
    };

interface ReadingProvider {
  read(request: ReadingRequestV0_1): Promise<ReadingCandidateSetV0_1>;
}

interface OeditExportAdapter {
  import(envelope: OeditGenesisEnvelopeV0_1): Promise<{
    candidateSet: ReadingCandidateSetV0_1;
    importReceipt: OeditImportReceiptV0_1;
  }>;
}
```

The binding sequence is:

```text
SourceEnvelope + pre-call processing basis
  → ReadingProvider
  → ReadingCandidateSetV0_1
  → exact-anchor validation
  → HumanAdmissionV0_1
  → PinecoeneScore
  → DisclosureProjection
  → PerformanceScore
  → OfferingPackage
  → Witness Player
```

Showcase V0 implements:

- `FixtureReadingProvider` for PCN-0002;
- `GenesisLiteReadingProvider` for bounded model-assisted proposals;
- optional `ManualReadingProvider` as fallback and test oracle.

A provider may output candidates only. It cannot output owner admission, Muse acknowledgment, Address, Offering authority, external truth, release, or Seal.

Future integration uses the separate `OeditExportAdapter` shown above. It accepts only a versioned, immutable, owner-authorized `OeditGenesisEnvelopeV0_1` carrying exact source and upstream object hashes, coverage, human dispositions, rights/disclosure ceiling, purpose, OPEN items, lineage, standing, and export authorization. It emits a candidate set plus a hash-bound import receipt with an exact upstream-disposition-to-candidate map, rejected-disposition exclusions, upstream standing, accepted purpose, rights hash, and effective disclosure ceiling. Showcase V0 has no live Œdit integration. The adapter imports no Œdit database model, session, internal identity, Blob path, provider grant, workflow lease, or mutable latest state. It cannot mutate Œdit or silently upgrade imported standing; any local selection or rewrite that does not preserve the mapped upstream dispositions is labeled owner-curated from a governed import.

### 6.4 Route and trust-zone model

```text
/                     public editorial entry and Flagship doorway
/make                 standalone Genesis Lite
/studio/:id           browser-local owner review and expression
/w/:id                hosted flagship encounter or same-browser local Preview
/api/genesis/read     stateless bounded provider route; no custody or product database
/lab                   development-only renderer laboratory
```

The route must identify whether `/w/:id` is a hosted fixture or local Preview. Closed content and every unpermitted public or protected field are removed before the player receives its Offering package. Explicitly permitted protected content may enter only within the effective ceiling, Address, resolution, and selected permissions; disallowed material is never shipped and hidden in state, CSS, shaders, inaccessible controls, or source maps.

Before `/api/genesis/read` receives source bytes, the browser canonicalizes the exact provider-request payload, computes its expected request hash, and stores a single-use authorization bound to that digest, source-envelope hash, provider/version, method, purpose, data-policy identifier, and timestamp. The route recomputes and validates all of them with same origin, schema, 16 KiB, rate, and concurrency limits before invoking a server-only provider credential. It stores no source or response body, excludes both from logs, analytics, traces, and errors, makes no automatic retry, and returns candidates only. Policy mismatch, hash mismatch, timeout, malformed output, rate limit, or anchor failure fails closed into the manual path.

The canonical origin is `https://pinecoene.com`. All other production aliases redirect before origin-scoped local custody is created.

### 6.5 Local-first boundary

- Pinecœne creation and saving require no account.
- Encountering the hosted flagship requires no account.
- Browser-local Pinecœnes are origin-, browser-, and device-scoped.
- Clearing site storage may remove them unless exported.
- Source remains local unless the owner authorizes one declared provider request or export.
- private `*.pcn.owner.json` backup/import proves owner-custody portability;
- recipient `*.pcn.offer.json` proves a distinct recipient-safe player-input boundary and must contain no private source, candidate, admission-detail, or private-Muse state.
- Local Open, Decline, and Return are not delivery receipts or server-observed recipient acts.
- Preview and Witness use one serialized Offering package and one actual player.

The optional relay and authenticated custody are post-showcase products. They do not become semantic truth or a requirement for canonical playback.

**Gate E4:** the exact hosted artifact proves the bounded Showcase V0 claims. READY, a hosted route, or visual polish alone is insufficient.

2. network-free `.pcn.html` and deterministic archive;

## 9 · Working cadence and decision discipline

- Review visual work against the frozen fixture, not general taste alone.
- Keep a visible decision log for material, interaction, authority, standing, and technical choices.
- Maintain one real/simplified/fixture/local/deferred copy ledger.
- Preserve one known-good fixture and adversarial variants across every layer.
- Every milestone ends with evidence appropriate to its claims.
- Use Vercel Previews for coded checkpoints without treating Preview as acceptance or release.
- Do not broaden source types, providers, routes, or storage while a current gate is unresolved.
- Stage only intended files and preserve supplied concept material unchanged.
- A named human accepts the selected art direction, Showcase Conformance Profile, hosted candidate, exact Offering package, and any later release separately. Each acceptance record names one scope and the exact subject hash; acceptance of one scope, revision, deployment, package, or release never transfers to another. Exact Offering-package acceptance is recorded only after compilation in a later external evidence-ledger revision and is not embedded back into the package it accepts.

## 10 · Indicative delivery

For one focused senior implementation, with E0/E1 protocol work allowed before material-direction selection and visual implementation beginning after it:

- **2–4 days** — fixture chain, Showcase Conformance Profile, workspace, and golden tests;
- **7–10 days** — Flagship object, opening, inspection, and fallback;
- **5–7 days** — Make Your Own, Genesis Lite, local custody, Preview, and JSON round-trip;
- **4–6 days** — accessibility, browser, mobile performance, truthful copy, and deployment verification.

A convincing public showcase is approximately **3–5 focused weeks after material direction is selected**, or **5–7 weeks** while material direction remains exploratory. This is an indicative planning range, not a delivery commitment.

The production-complete vertical slice remains a separate approximately **12–16 week** program. Showcase work advances that program because its protocol, engine, player, fixture, accessibility paths, and object grammar are production-directed rather than disposable.
