## 0 · Revision decision

Version 0.6 preserves the relational architecture and experience laws of v0.5 while defining a bounded, standalone-capable Showcase V0 that may be built before the full production system or a live Œdit bridge.

It adds:

- **Showcase V0** — a truthful, weeks-scale proof of the complete Pinecœne thesis before the larger production commitment;
- **Two-entry experience** — one art-directed flagship Offering and one lightweight make-your-own path;
- **Standalone Genesis** — Pinecœne creation without an Œdit account or live Œdit dependency;
- **Genesis Reader V0.1** — a bounded, source-grounded proposal reader for short text and Markdown;
- **Human Admission** — every event and Fold relation is disposed and every Muse and Muse relation is separately acknowledged or rejected before compilation;
- **Replaceable Reading Boundary** — fixture, manual, and lightweight-model providers converge on one candidate contract; a future Œdit envelope adapter emits the same contract plus an import receipt;
- **Standing and Evidence** — immutable origin, reading, rights, and external-truth limits remain distinct from mutable implementation, custody, delivery, acceptance, release, and Seal evidence;
- **Œdit Bridge Profile** — a future offline-first adapter for an immutable, owner-authorized Œdit export rather than shared databases or runtime state;
- **Showcase Truth Boundary** — real, simplified, fixture-authored, local-only, unavailable, and future capabilities are named explicitly;
- **Small Physical Architecture** — the first build preserves canonical boundaries without prematurely creating the full production package graph.

The visual thesis in this revision remains a strong default candidate. It may be refined after concept review, but no concept may weaken provenance, disclosure, accessibility, immutability, or owner authority. The flagship specimen may be curated to guarantee first-sight quality; its authored or fixture standing must remain visible and must not be presented as general machine understanding.

> **Pinecœne is a medium through which intelligence in action can acquire a portable body, travel with provenance and boundaries intact, and become generative in an encounter with another intelligence.**

This revision does not create a third canonical product profile. Showcase V0 is an implementation and evidence boundary, not a new semantic grammar.

Where this document says **Showcase Conformance Profile**, it means the bounded v0.1 contract and test subset of the Fold Profile. It is not a third product profile and cannot change Fold semantics.

The normative implementation-status axis is:

- `specified` — described in an accepted or candidate document only;
- `fixture_authored` — frozen specimen data prepared for demonstration or testing;
- `prototype_only` — interactive behavior without the complete product contract or proof layer;
- `implemented_local` — executable locally with bounded verification;
- `implemented_hosted` — reachable in a named hosted environment;
- `verified` — the exact claimed behavior has current evidence.

Human acceptance is separately `not_claimed` or a non-empty set of scoped `accepted` records; release is `not_released`, `release_candidate`, or `released`; Seal is `not_sealed` or `sealed`. Every accepted, release-candidate, released, or sealed claim binds its exact subject hash as well as its authority and evidence where required. These axes do not imply one another. A polished prototype, passing build, hosted route, or READY deployment is not human acceptance, release, or Seal.

The system still has two profiles:

1. **Fold Profile** — produces persistent owner-held Pinecœnes.
2. **7 Presence Profile** — produces session-scoped face, voice, and accountable assembly telemetry.

Constellation, Address, Animation, Offering, Encounter, Return, and Successor are layers and lifecycle stages around the Fold Profile.

> **One grammar. Two profiles. One relational lifecycle.**

## 1 · Canonical product model

### 1.1 Pinecœne

A **Pinecœne** is an owner-held, versioned, executable conformation compiled from:

- an owner-authorized record;
- a declared reading of that record;
- explicit standing and permissions;
- owner-recognized formative lineage;
- and an owner admission event.

It may be rendered through space, sound, text, story, touch, or another conformant medium.

It is not:

- a direct picture of a mind;
- a personality portrait;
- a psychometric profile;
- a contribution score;
- a decorative data visualization;
- a raw transcript;
- a chain-of-thought display;
- a claim that one true geometry was hidden inside the source.

### 1.2 The larger act

A Pinecœne is the portable object inside a larger act of accountable passage.

The canonical lifecycle is:

```text
RECORD
  → SOURCE-PROCESSING AUTHORIZATION WHEN AN EXTERNAL READER IS USED
  → READING PROPOSAL
  → HUMAN DISPOSITION / ADMISSION
  → RECOGNITION / CONSTELLATION
  → AUTHORIZED SCORE
  → FOLD
  → ANIMATION / EXPRESSION
  → ADDRESS
  → OFFERING
  → ENCOUNTER
  → RETURN
  → SUCCESSOR
```

The lifecycle may stop at any stage. A machine draft may remain a draft. Human review may remain incomplete. Neither condition creates a Fold.

A Fold may remain private.

An Address may remain latent.

An Offering may be declined.

A Return may remain OPEN.

A successor may never be admitted.

No stage is compulsory merely because a previous stage occurred.

### 1.3 Four human acts around compilation

The owner performs two acts before compilation and two after it.

#### Admission

Before compilation, the owner decides which source-grounded reading candidates may shape the Fold. A candidate may be kept, corrected and kept, left OPEN, or removed. Machine confidence, recurrence, visual opportunity, or an Œdit import cannot substitute for the required owner act at the standing declared by the input profile.

#### Recognition

Before compilation, the owner confirms which Muses and formative relations mattered. Candidate, rejected, and unreviewed Muses remain outside the canonical score.

The compiler then produces a lawful body from admitted events, owner-left-OPEN events, and acknowledged Muses only.

#### Animation

The owner chooses how the body should live, sound, and feel without changing semantic topology.

#### Address

The owner chooses where the form is pointed and what may open there.

These acts are not machine authorization.

The system may propose candidates. Only the owner may admit, recognize, animate, address, and offer the Fold as theirs. An imported governed Œdit may carry prior human dispositions, but Pinecœne still requires a local owner act authorizing this exact Fold and disclosure purpose.

### 1.4 Experience thesis — The Offered Object

The first experience of Pinecœne is not a dashboard, an ontology lesson, or a visualization demo.

It is the encounter with a quiet, folded object that was intentionally offered by one intelligence to another.

At first sight the object should feel:

- intimate rather than spectacular;
- materially credible rather than graphically decorative;
- composed rather than ambiently busy;
- strange enough to invite attention;
- calm enough to carry trust;
- inspectable enough to deserve belief.

The preferred visual world is a folded reliquary under raking light: paper, mineral, glass, metal, shadow, seam, and aperture. The experience may feel quietly impossible, but it must never feel causally arbitrary.

Pinecœne is not represented by:

- a generic glowing orb;
- a particle nebula;
- a cybernetic heads-up display;
- a literal pinecone;
- decorative artificial-intelligence theater;
- permanent breathing, orbiting, or idle motion that implies life without an event.

The first expressive promise is:

> **This form exists because something happened, someone chose what it meant, and someone chose to offer it to you.**

The product introduces canonical vocabulary progressively. A recipient may first encounter only:

- Pinecœne;
- Muse;
- Locket;
- Return.

Core, Bag, Quarta, Trail, Şekli, Standing, and the complete compiler grammar become available through inspection and Studio use. The system does not require a recipient to learn the ontology before receiving value.

## 2 · Profiles and surfaces

### 2.1 Fold Profile

The Fold Profile produces a persistent Pinecœne.

Eligible subjects include:

- a completed human Œdit;
- a human-authored reading of a source;
- a document or decision;
- a project or product history;
- a Git repository and its admitted development record;
- a relationship or collaboration record with declared seats;
- a personal reflection;
- a work composed for another person.

Canonical Fold objects:

- Trail;
- Şekli;
- Glyph;
- Locket;
- Constellation;
- Replay;
- Whistle;
- Recital;
- Passport.

The Fold Profile has multiple lawful genesis origins:

- a standalone owner-curated Pinecœne Genesis;
- a manual reading authored by the owner;
- a frozen fixture whose authored standing is declared;
- a future owner-authorized governed Œdit export;
- another versioned provider that satisfies the candidate and admission contracts.

Œdit is therefore an optional higher-assurance reading provider. It is never an account prerequisite, identity provider, database dependency, or mandatory runtime for Pinecœne.

### 2.2 7 Presence Profile

7 Presence renders observable runtime state as a compact audiovisual identity.

It does not produce:

- a Pinecœne owned by 7;
- a human Fold;
- a personal Muse Constellation;
- a Locket belonging to 7;
- hidden chain-of-thought;
- an automatically persistent memory.

### 2.3 Product surfaces

The complete implementation should expose five primary surfaces. Showcase V0 may expose a smaller route set while preserving the same object and authority boundaries.

#### Genesis Workbench

Receives an exact short source, invokes a declared reading provider, validates exact anchors, and asks the owner to dispose every event and Fold-relation candidate and separately acknowledge or reject Muse and Muse-relation candidates. It is a bounded entrance to Fold Studio, not an Œdit substitute.

#### Fold Studio

Imports and reads the record, preserves source/read separation, supports human encounter, and compiles the candidate Fold.

#### Constellation Pass

Lets the owner review candidate Muses, acknowledge formative lineage, link prior artifacts, control identity and visibility, and reject machine proposals.

#### Offering Canvas

Lets the owner animate the Fold, choose Address, choose resolution, preview the recipient experience, and create an Offering.

#### Witness Player

Lets a recipient encounter the Offering at the granted resolution, without mutating the Fold.

#### 7 Presence

Makes active session state socially legible and performs the handoff between open session and persistent owner Fold.

### 2.4 Showcase V0 surfaces

Showcase V0 must prove the thesis through two connected paths:

1. **Flagship Encounter** — one frozen, art-directed, inspectable Pinecœne with one stable recipient route and one declared artifact standing.
2. **Make Your Own** — a local-first short-text journey through Genesis Reader V0.1, owner review, Fold compilation, Expression, and exact Preview.

The flagship carries this controlling disclosure:

> **This specimen is curated fixture content. Its sender, recipient, admission, opening, consent, and Return states do not establish that those human acts occurred, that delivery happened, or that an external truth was verified.**

Its deterministic rendering and inspectable causality may be real while its semantic map remains authored and frozen. It is not evidence of general document understanding or a geometry discovered by a machine.

The minimum showcase route family is:

```text
/
/make
/studio/:pinecoeneId
/w/:offeringId
/api/genesis/read
```

The flagship must not depend on a model call at encounter time. Make Your Own may use a declared provider only after explicit source-processing authorization.

A stable hosted flagship route may be public. A locally generated `/w/:offeringId` remains a same-browser Preview. A recipient-safe exported file may be transferred only as a file and must not create a delivery receipt; the local route itself is never described as a delivered or shareable recipient link.

## 3 · Foundational distinctions

### 3.1 Source

What actually exists in the record:

- text;
- message;
- commit;
- diff;
- test;
- receipt;
- image;
- event;
- annotation;
- exact source locus.

### 3.2 Reading

A declared interpretation of what a source event is doing.

Examples:

- proposal;
- return;
- repair;
- collision;
- direct address;
- delegation;
- evaluation;
- withheld advancement;
- formative resonance.

The reading is an act and remains contestable.

### 3.3 Standing

What status the owner and instrument currently assign:

- observed;
- candidate;
- contested;
- acknowledged;
- admitted;
- rejected;
- OPEN;
- sealed.

### 3.4 Receipt provenance

Why a claim or relation may stand.

### 3.5 Assembly provenance

Which event, transition, or rule produced a feature of the Pinecœne.

### 3.6 Formative lineage

What entered the maker’s field and altered, accompanied, or reoriented the becoming.

Formative lineage is not automatically evidentiary provenance.

### 3.7 Expression

How the owner chooses to perform the compiled form.

Expression does not change standing.

### 3.8 Address

Where the owner points the Offering.

Address does not grant access.

### 3.9 Offering

The bounded act of presenting one exact Fold version through one exact Expression Profile, Address, and disclosure resolution.

### 3.10 Encounter

The recipient’s permitted experience of the Offering.

### 3.11 Return

A new response arriving from the recipient or world.

### 3.12 Successor

A new Pinecœne version produced from admitted subsequent record events.

A successor never mutates its predecessor.

### 3.13 Genesis origin

The declared process by which an exact source and proposed reading entered Pinecœne.

Canonical origin kinds are:

- `fixture_authored`;
- `owner_manual`;
- `genesis_lite`;
- `oedit_export`;
- `other_versioned_provider`.

Origin describes provenance and method. It does not upgrade standing.

### 3.14 Candidate reading

A source-grounded, non-authoritative proposal produced by a declared reader. Every candidate carries exact source anchors, provider identity, uncertainty, coverage, omissions, and `candidate` standing.

No reading provider may emit owner admission, Muse acknowledgment, Address, Offering authority, release, or Seal.

### 3.15 Human admission

The explicit owner decision that determines whether a candidate becomes Fold material.

Allowed decisions are:

- `keep`;
- `rewrite_and_keep`;
- `leave_open`;
- `remove`.

There is no bulk acceptance shortcut in Genesis Reader V0.1. Unreviewed and removed candidates remain outside the admitted semantic digest and cannot affect topology, sound, motion, disclosure, or hashes derived from admitted content.

### 3.16 Artifact standing

Pinecœne separates immutable origin standing from mutable evidence about implementation and real-world events. Neither may be inflated.

Showcase V0 must distinguish:

- exact source bytes observed;
- rights declared by the owner but not independently verified;
- machine-provisional reading;
- owner-curated reading;
- governed imported reading;
- external truth not established;
- not an Œdit;
- implementation, custody, and delivery as separately evidenced;
- human acceptance, release, and Seal only when separately authorized and evidenced.

## 4 · Shared material grammar

The material grammar remains:

- **Solid**
- **Particulate**
- **Edge**

The substances may be interpreted across visual, sonic, textual, and physical renderers. Their semantic distinction must survive.

### 4.1 Solid

Committed standing.

In a Fold, Solid may represent:

- admitted aim;
- admitted operation;
- standing proposition;
- owner seal;
- admitted Quarta.

In 7 Presence, Solid remains ghostlike and provisional.

Machine proposals, unreviewed readings, removed candidates, unresolved source anchors, and decorative Showcase features may not acquire Solid volume. Owner-left-OPEN material may shape a visible opening or boundary but may not counterfeit committed standing.

### 4.2 Particulate

Plural external presences.

Particulate contains two explicitly distinct semantic classes:

#### Return particulate

What reality answered:

- receipt;
- objection;
- test;
- witness;
- contradiction;
- confirmation;
- missing coverage;
- recipient return.

#### Muse particulate

What the owner recognizes as having entered or accompanied the becoming:

- person;
- living being;
- work;
- place;
- memory;
- music;
- image;
- object;
- idea;
- event;
- prior Pinecœne.

Return and Muse particulate must not be visually or sonically conflated.

A Muse cannot strengthen standing merely by proximity.

### 4.3 Edge

Boundary and relation.

Edge may represent:

- source boundary;
- Bag;
- scope;
- version;
- exclusion;
- permission;
- Locket state;
- relation between events;
- relation between a Muse and the Fold;
- relation between Muses;
- Address vector;
- operative boundary;
- successor relation.

### 4.4 Quarta

A fourth-point relation that creates a new dimensional possibility.

Quarta is not:

- a label for every insight;
- a visual reward;
- a fourth list item;
- a brightness increase;
- a generic AI “aha.”

Only owner-admitted Quarta may acquire committed volume.

### 4.5 Openness

OPEN must remain perceivable.

- no coverage stays sparse;
- unresolved relation stays unclosed;
- withheld advancement stays withheld;
- an open record never receives a false cadence;
- an unanswered Address remains unanswered;
- a declined Offering is not rendered as rejection of the owner.

### 4.6 Charge

Charge is computed from active receipt binding and freshness.

Charge may affect luminance, resonance, vitality, or another renderer-specific parameter.

Charge may not be manually increased by the Expression Profile.

## 6 · Muse and Constellation specification

### 6.1 Muse definition

A **Muse** is an owner-recognized formative presence that entered, accompanied, resonated with, or reoriented the becoming of a Fold.

A Muse is not automatically:

- evidence;
- causal proof;
- authorization;
- attribution;
- endorsement;
- co-authorship;
- a recipient.

### 6.2 Muse kinds

Version 0.6 supports:

```text
person
living_being
place
work
memory
music
image
object
idea
event
prior_pinecoene
other
```

### 6.3 Muse standing and facts

Muse disposition is a single owner-governed state:

```text
candidate
acknowledged
rejected
```

#### Candidate

Machine- or human-proposed but not owner-recognized.

#### Acknowledged

The owner confirms formative relevance.

#### Rejected

The owner rejects the proposed relation.

Mention and linkage are orthogonal facts, not higher standing:

- `mentionedInRecord` says the Muse appears explicitly in the authorized record;
- `linkedArtifactRef` connects the Muse to an inspectable source or artifact;
- `linkedPinecoeneId` connects the Muse to a separate Pinecœne;
- none of these facts acknowledges the Muse on the owner’s behalf.

### 6.4 Muse relations

Version 0.6 supports:

```text
thought_of
remembered
reminded_by
resonated_with
learned_from
grew_from
grew_among
accompanied_by
reoriented_by
resembled
returned_to
connected_through
inspired_by
witnessed_by
```

Relation names are owner-visible and contestable.

### 6.5 Candidate generation

The system may propose a semantic event, relation, or Muse only when grounded in exact source anchors or an explicit owner-authored basis. Every model-proposed anchor must resolve byte-exactly inside a declared source container. Fuzzy or closest-passage fallback is prohibited.

The system may propose a Muse only when grounded in:

- an explicit record mention;
- a source link;
- an owner prompt;
- a prior declared relation.

The system must not infer hidden emotional attachments from behavior alone.

Candidate generation never creates admission, acknowledgment, Address, Offering authority, external truth, release, or Seal. Unresolved anchors are excluded before owner review and cannot enter the score.

### 6.6 Constellation topology

A Constellation is not a ranking.

Do not compute or display:

- “most important person”;
- influence leaderboard;
- emotional score;
- centrality as value;
- psychological diagnosis.

Permitted structural properties include:

- first appearance;
- recurrence;
- relation type;
- source link;
- event attachment;
- continuity across successors;
- visibility state.

### 6.7 Rendering law

Muses remain outside committed Core standing.

They may:

- orbit;
- recur;
- form constellations;
- connect to events;
- connect to other Muses;
- point to prior Pinecœnes;
- remain faint or private.

They may not:

- fill the Core;
- increase charge;
- masquerade as receipts;
- close an OPEN relation;
- acquire owner identity without permission.

Candidate and rejected Muses are Studio-only review material. They are excluded from the canonical Constellation, the admitted semantic digest, recipient packages, topology seeds, visual rendering, and sound rendering.

### 6.8 Constellation disclosure

The owner may independently choose:

- whether the Constellation is visible;
- which Muse identities are named;
- which are aliased;
- which remain abstract;
- which relations open;
- whether linked artifacts may be followed.

### 7.4 Address law

## 8 · Animation and Expression Profile

### 8.1 Definition

**Animation** is the human act of giving the compiled Pinecœne mood, dedication, sensory character, and presentation.

The technical object is an **Expression Profile**.

Animation does not mean merely adding motion. It uses the older sense of giving life or breath to a body.

### 8.2 Allowed controls

The owner may control:

- semantic-role hue mapping;
- particle tint and sparkle family;
- background or stage;
- reflected-light atmosphere;
- material finish;
- motion temperament;
- transition pacing within semantic timing bounds;
- sonic timbre;
- instrument family;
- spatialization;
- cover crop;
- frame;
- title;
- inscription;
- dedication;
- accessibility alternatives.

### 8.3 Protected architecture

The Expression Profile may not:

- change semantic topology;
- add or remove Core vertices;
- fabricate Quarta;
- convert candidate to admitted;
- hide meaningful gaps;
- alter closure state;
- increase charge;
- change source or reading;
- remove provenance;
- turn a Muse into a receipt;
- turn an Address into permission;
- change Locket resolution;
- mutate a sealed Fold.

### 8.4 Semantic-role color law

The owner may change hues.

The renderer must preserve distinction among:

- Solid;
- Return particulate;
- Muse particulate;
- Edge;
- OPEN;
- Charge.

Hue may vary. Semantic contrast may not collapse.

### 8.5 Motion temperament

Version 0.6 may support bounded presets:

```text
still
calm
tender
playful
solemn
bright
restless
ceremonial
```

A preset changes performance parameters, not event meaning.

No preset may make unresolved material appear resolved.

### 8.6 Sonic expression

The owner may choose timbre and instrumentation.

The event-to-sound relation remains fixed by the Sonic Profile.

A return remains a return.

A gap remains a withheld event.

Quarta remains a relational change rather than a louder sound.

### 8.7 Multiple Expression Profiles

A Fold may have many Expression Profiles.

Each profile is versioned and hashable.

Changing Expression creates a new presentation, not a successor Fold.

## 9 · Offering Canvas

### 9.1 Purpose

The Offering Canvas is the owner surface where a lawful Fold becomes a relational offering.

### 9.2 Canvas stages

#### Stage 1 — Recognize

- review candidate Muses;
- confirm, reject, alias, or hide;
- link prior Pinecœnes or source artifacts;
- inspect formative lineage.

#### Stage 2 — Animate

- choose palette;
- choose particle treatment;
- choose stage and light;
- choose motion temperament;
- choose sonic instrumentation;
- add title or dedication.

#### Stage 3 — Address

- select recipient or audience;
- write optional address text;
- confirm recipient privacy;
- choose whether Address is visible.

#### Stage 4 — Open

- choose disclosure resolution;
- choose Constellation visibility;
- choose source and Trail visibility;
- choose sound and replay permissions;
- choose download and persistence behavior.

#### Stage 5 — Preview

Compile and preview exactly what the recipient will receive:

- compile the exact disclosure projection and selected permissions;
- finalize the Expression, performance, evidence snapshot, sender disclosure, mode, expiry, player version, and sorted asset manifest;
- compute the cycle-free Offering-intent hash over every recipient-visible pre-authorization input;
- complete the intent-bound right-to-offer authorization and any required blocking consent review;
- compile the immutable Offering package candidate;
- preview those exact serialized bytes through the Witness Player.

The preview must include:

- visual;
- sound;
- reduced-motion;
- sound-off;
- protected-content check;
- Address disclosure check.

#### Stage 6 — Offer

Commit the already-previewed package to the selected action: save it, export it, or activate an available transport capability. Stage 6 does not recompile or mutate the package. Any change to Address, permissions, Expression, evidence snapshot, assets, or authorization returns to Stage 5 and creates a new package hash before another Preview.

### 9.3 Canvas law

The Canvas may animate the body.

It may not edit the earned architecture.

Any request to change semantic topology routes back to Fold Studio and creates a candidate successor workflow.

### 9.4 Progressive authoring law

The complete authoring lifecycle may expose the full grammar gradually. The first thin slice should organize the owner’s work around seven human moments:

1. **What happened?** — select or create the authorized record.
2. **What might it mean?** — inspect source-grounded reading candidates and uncertainty.
3. **What enters?** — dispose every event and Fold relation; separately acknowledge or reject every Muse and Muse relation.
4. **What shaped this?** — review and acknowledge Muses separately from evidence.
5. **How should it feel?** — animate the lawful body.
6. **Who is this for?** — confirm Address and disclosure.
7. **What may they receive?** — preview and offer.

Canonical terms remain visible in inspectable detail, documentation, and advanced controls. Progressive disclosure may simplify language; it may not collapse distinctions or hide authority changes.

The Preview stage must use the same Witness Player and the exact hash-consistent serialized `OfferingPackage` that a recipient-safe export receives. A separate imitation preview or app-private performance state is non-conformant.

Offering Canvas cannot repair missing source grounding, human admission, Muse recognition, or rights declarations. Those conditions must be complete before an Offering can be compiled.

## 10 · Sharing and consent architecture

### 10.1 Resolutions

#### Resolution 0 — Glyph

Closed projection only.

#### Resolution 1 — Card

Glyph plus owner-selected metadata, title, and dedication.

#### Resolution 2 — Form

Full geometry and sound without protected proposition or receipt content.

#### Resolution 3 — Inspect

Selected source, reading, Muse, boundary, and return details.

#### Resolution 4 — Replay

Permitted Trail and becoming.

#### Resolution 5 — Recital

Ephemeral performed encounter with no compulsory persistent copy.

Resolution is a maximum disclosure ceiling, not a grant that overrides narrower permissions. Effective disclosure is the intersection of source visibility, owner standing, Muse visibility, Address visibility, resolution, Offering permissions, consent constraints, and recipient accessibility needs. When rules conflict, the most restrictive lawful rule wins and the Preview must explain the narrowing.

### 10.2 Offering modes

```text
glyph
locket
open_hands
recital
```

### 10.3 Offering properties

An Offering binds:

- Pinecœne ID;
- Fold version;
- Expression Profile ID;
- Address ID;
- resolution;
- Muse visibility;
- Trail visibility;
- sound permission;
- replay permission;
- download policy;
- expiry;
- revocation status.

### 10.4 Decline law

A recipient may decline without penalty.

Decline does not:

- lower owner standing;
- create a negative Muse relation;
- create a failure mark;
- imply rejection of the owner;
- become public.

### 10.5 Locket law

Recognition never creates entitlement.

Seeing a Glyph or Locket never grants Form, Inspect, Replay, or Recital access.

### 10.6 Carrier law

7 or another carrier may present the Offering.

The carrier may not:

- open protected resolution;
- inherit the Fold;
- inherit charge;
- alter Address;
- alter Expression;
- accept on behalf of the recipient.

### 10.7 Shared-material consent

Authorization occurs through three separate acts:

1. **Source processing** — before source bytes are sent to any model provider, the owner authorizes that exact processing and sees the declared provider boundary.
2. **Fold inclusion** — before compilation, the owner disposes every event and Fold-relation candidate and separately acknowledges or rejects every Muse and Muse-relation candidate.
3. **Right to offer** — before Offering compilation, the owner confirms the right to disclose the selected projection to the declared Address.

One authorization does not imply either of the others.

Before a Fold or Offering is compiled, the owner must declare whether the authorized record contains material created with or about another living person.

The declaration must distinguish at minimum:

- owner-only material;
- shared conversation or co-created material;
- quoted or linked public material;
- private third-party material;
- material involving a child or protected person;
- memorial material involving a deceased person.

This declaration does not automatically establish legal permission. It determines which consent checks and disclosure warnings the product must present.

The owner must explicitly confirm the right to offer the selected projection. The system must support exclusion, aliasing, narrowing, and withdrawal of third-party material without rewriting the sealed predecessor.

A recipient controls whether their Return is:

- private to the encounter;
- available to the owner as candidate successor material;
- exportable or linkable;
- later retractable where transport permits.

Silence is never consent. Relationship, familiarity, or prior receipt of an Offering is never blanket permission for future Offerings.

### 10.8 Canonical origin and transport honesty

The web product must have one canonical origin because browser-local custody is origin-scoped.

The first production origin is:

```text
https://pinecoene.com
```

`https://www.pinecoene.com`, `https://pinecœne.com`, and their equivalent punycode or `www` forms must permanently redirect to the canonical origin before application state is written.

Direct and relay transport must state their limits plainly:

- relay revocation can prevent future retrieval but cannot revoke a package already downloaded;
- `allowDownload: false` can suppress an offered download control but cannot prevent copying of bytes already delivered to a recipient device;
- Recital can avoid creating a compulsory persistent copy but cannot prevent screenshots, screen recording, photography, or human recollection;
- a self-contained direct package is intentionally usable without the relay and therefore cannot depend on later relay revocation.

The interface must not claim stronger control than the carrier can enforce.

### 11.1 Encounter

- rotate;
- listen;
- inspect permitted layers;


Silence is the absence of a Return. It is not serialized, treated as an observed recipient action, or assigned consent or standing.

### 11.3 Return admission

A recipient Return arrives as candidate record material.

The owner may:

- admit;
- reject;
- narrow;
- leave OPEN;
- attach as Muse;
- attach as receipt;
- attach as Address;
- exclude from successor.

The system must not decide the semantic class solely from message content.

### 11.4 Successor law

A sealed Fold never mutates.

If admitted returns change the record:

```text
predecessor Fold
  + admitted return events
  + declared reading
  + owner seal
  → successor Fold
```

The predecessor remains replayable and addressable.

### 11.5 Docking

A successor may dock beside its predecessor.

A returned Pinecœne may dock without merging.

> **Shapes may touch. They do not collapse into one another by default.**

### 11.6 Encounter Overture

The default recipient encounter should feel like receiving a letter, not entering software.

Before opening, the recipient sees:

- who sent the Offering, when disclosure permits;
- the dedication or Address text;
- the closed Glyph or Locket;
- a concise statement of what opening reveals and what the sender may learn;
- three quiet choices: **Open**, **Not now**, and **Decline**.

No account is required before this value is available. Sound never autoplays. Opening begins only after a recipient action.

The first thirty seconds should follow this score:

1. the closed object rests under raking light without decorative idle motion;
2. the recipient chooses to open;
3. lawful Edges separate and Solids acquire depth;
4. Return particulate arrives as discrete, directional events;
5. acknowledged Muses awaken at the periphery, never inside committed Core standing;
6. Address becomes an orientation of the whole composition;
7. any OPEN relation remains an unjoined seam, missing face, or unresolved cadence;
8. the object settles into an inspectable state.

Selecting any meaningful feature reveals an explanation appropriate to the Offering resolution:

- what semantic class the feature represents;
- which event or admitted relation caused it;
- what source, reading, and standing are permitted to be shown;
- why the feature remains open, bounded, or sealed.

This is **Inspectable Causality**. It is the primary source of wonder and trust. Visual complexity without causal legibility is non-conformant.

The v0.1 containerization profile is deliberately narrow: `containers` contains exactly one `body` entry spanning the source’s unchanged UTF-8 bytes and JavaScript UTF-16 code units. Line endings and Unicode are not normalized.   "pinecoene.reader-normalizer.v0.1",
```text
  { sourceSha256, fromMuseCandidateId, toMuseCandidateId, proposedType, proposedVisibility, sourceCandidateIds }
)
```

`museId` and an acknowledged Muse `relationId` are explicit owner-admission inputs rather than normalizer outputs; they obey the ID grammar and are frozen in golden fixtures. Existing normalized IDs survive provider array reordering and the addition of unrelated rejected proposals.

`SourceProcessingAuthorizationV0_1` is created, validated, and persisted locally before an external provider request. The browser first computes `expectedRequestHash = H("provider-request", providerVersion, { sourceEnvelope, providerId, providerVersion, providerDataPolicyId, methodHash, exactPurpose })`; the authorization then binds that digest and the independently computed source-envelope hash. Its hash excludes only `authorizationHash` itself. The server reconstructs the same payload and refuses any mismatch before provider invocation. `ReadingCandidateSetV0_1.provider.requestHash` must equal the authorized expected hash; `responseHash` binds the exact validated structured provider response before normalization. `genesis_lite` and `other_versioned_provider` require `source_processing_authorization`; fixture, manual, and validated offline Œdit import require their matching `no_external_processing` reason. Provider kind, policy, request hash, response hash, method hash, and processing-basis hash must agree or the candidate set is invalid.

An admission is closed only when each event, Fold relation, Muse, and Muse relation candidate has exactly one known decision and each decision names a member of that candidate set. Duplicate and foreign IDs fail validation. Acknowledged Muse relations are valid only when both endpoint Muses were acknowledged. Fixture admissions use `fixture_authored`; that label records curated fixture data and does not assert an observed human action.

The standalone Genesis projector applies one deterministic mapping: `keep` creates an admitted event or relation using the candidate values; `rewrite_and_keep` creates an admitted event or relation using the owner-authored replacement; `leave_open` creates an OPEN event or relation; and `remove` creates nothing canonical. Acknowledged Muses and Muse relations enter `admittedConstellation`; rejected candidates do not. Candidate review material never enters the score.

`ArtifactStandingV0_1` is immutable origin, reading, rights, and external-truth standing. Implementation, custody, delivery, human acceptance, release, and Seal are mutable real-world claims held in a separately versioned `ArtifactEvidenceLedgerV0_1`; they never alter `genesisHash`, `scoreHash`, or `semanticHash`. Every acceptance record applies only to its `(scope, subjectHash)` pair: fixture semantics bind the frozen fixture-manifest hash; art direction binds the selected study/decision artifact hash; Showcase conformance binds the exact profile hash; hosted-candidate acceptance binds a deployment-evidence manifest hash covering Git revision, deployment identity, and canonical URL; Offering-package acceptance binds the already-compiled `packageHash`; and release-candidate acceptance binds the release-manifest hash. Records sort by ASCII scope and then subject hash. Acceptance never transfers to another scope, revision, deployment, package, or release. An Offering-package acceptance is necessarily written to a later external evidence-ledger revision and is never embedded back into the package it accepts, which would create a circular hash. `accepted`, `released`, and `sealed` require named authority and evidence references. A fixture, local build, hosted route, or visually finished object cannot self-promote into those states. `PinecoeneGenesisV0_1.standing.originKind` is the single origin authority; duplicate origin fields are prohibited.

The future Œdit bridge consumes `OeditGenesisEnvelopeV0_1`, not a generic string or live product state, and terminates at `ReadingCandidateSetV0_1` plus `OeditImportReceiptV0_1`. Each imported candidate anchor is reconstructed only from a represented envelope locus and its exact bytes, offsets, and hash. `upstreamStanding` is permitted only for `provider.kind: "oedit_export"` and its hash must match the receipt’s `upstreamStandingHash`.

The v0.1 adapter is one-to-one and total over upstream dispositions: every `admitted` disposition maps to exactly one candidate with `proposedPersistence: "fold"` and `preservingAction: "keep"`; every `open` disposition maps to exactly one candidate with `proposedPersistence: "open"` and `preservingAction: "leave_open"`; every `rejected` disposition maps to no candidate and appears exactly once in `excludedRejectedDispositionIds`. Every non-rejected mapping binds upstream disposition, event, and locus IDs to the normalized candidate ID. The adapter emits no event candidate without this upstream provenance and, because the v0.1 envelope has no canonical relation or Muse mapping surface, emits empty Fold-relation, Muse, and Muse-relation candidate arrays. Those require a successor envelope version. Duplicate, missing, extra, or cross-envelope mappings fail import.

Final Œdit-derived standing is computed after local admission. It remains `governed_import` only if every imported candidate receives its declared preserving action and none is rewritten or removed. Any rewrite, removal, admitted-to-OPEN change, OPEN-to-admitted change, or other local semantic selection yields `owner_curated_from_governed_import` and `governed_oedit_source_with_local_derivation`; upstream governance and constraints remain visible, but the locally derived reading is never attributed to Œdit. Pinecœne does not import Œdit database rows, storage locations, sessions, internal user IDs, workflow leases, provider grants, or mutable "latest" pointers.

Showcase V0 has no live Œdit integration. A future bridge may import an immutable, owner-authorized export snapshot into Pinecœne’s candidate-reading contract. It will not share databases, sessions, identities, provider grants, workflow leases, or mutable latest state, and it will not mutate Œdit.

`originKind: "oedit_export"` is valid only when the accepted profile, envelope hash, upstream object hashes, declared coverage, human dispositions, rights envelope, export authorization, import mappings, upstream-standing hash, and import-receipt hash validate together. At import, the receipt’s accepted purpose, rights-envelope hash, and effective disclosure ceiling must equal the corresponding envelope constraints exactly; the adapter cannot broaden or reinterpret them. Purpose order is `private_fold_only < recipient_offering < public_offering`: private prohibits Offering compilation, recipient permits only a non-public Address, and public permits either recipient or public Address. Disclosure ceilings order from `none` to `public_only` to `public_and_protected`; compilation takes the most restrictive intersection of upstream ceiling, local permission, and Address and never exports `closed` source. The local admission purpose may narrow but never broaden the imported purpose. A score or Offering that violates these constraints fails validation.

All terminal self-hashes exclude only their own named hash field. A machine-provisional Œdit Primer candidate may not reuse governed-import standing. Unknown profiles and unsupported versions fail closed.

`DisclosureProjection.conformation` is a newly constructed `RecipientSafeConformationV0_1`, not a redacted owner-held conformation. It must not retain disallowed nodes, strings, IDs, source references, authority hashes, or transitions from the owner-held conformation. Its explicit recipient-safe bag contains no source-manifest ID, owner-seat ID, Genesis hash, admission hash, or artifact-standing reference. `permittedPredecessorRef` is absent unless predecessor lineage is deliberately included by the resulting Disclosure Profile; it is a recipient-safe reference, never a copied private repository/storage identifier. Permitted standing and authority appear only through `originDisclosure`.

`semanticBasis` is the complete, serializable input to recipient-safe semantic hashing and topology compilation. A recipient event exposes only its recipient-safe event ID, sequence, operator, standing, timing, minimum resolution, and an optional explicitly owner-approved public label. It has no source coordinates, raw source, seat ID, unrestricted reading note/class, subject/target IDs, or arbitrary relation string. Recipient Fold relations may reference only event IDs present in the basis. Recipient Muses use an explicitly approved public label and separately permitted portable link references; copied owner-only link IDs and Genesis provenance are impossible in the type. Every public label is at most 160 exact UTF-8 bytes, contains no control characters, and may reproduce source words or private identity only when that exact content is permitted by the Disclosure Profile. Events retain canonical sequence order; Fold relations sort by ASCII relation ID; Muse nodes and Muse relations sort by ASCII ID. All references must resolve within the permitted basis or its explicit permitted-link set. No recipient topology input may be recovered from omitted owner state.

At resolutions 3–5, each record maps one visible feature to one or more IDs present in the recipient semantic basis/topology and carries only the selected source, reading, standing, boundary, or Return detail permitted at that resolution. Every `sourceRef`, `readingRef`, and `standingRef` must appear in the corresponding `DisclosureProfile.permitted*Refs`; those references are newly constructed recipient-safe aliases, not copied owner source, repository, storage, admission, or seat identifiers. Protected excerpts are absent unless the effective disclosure ceiling and selected Address permit protected content. Exact excerpts are byte-preserved, `exactExcerptSha256` equals lowercase-hex SHA-256 of the unnormalized UTF-8 excerpt, and each excerpt is limited to 2 KiB. `excerptId` is `xpt_` plus `H("recipient-excerpt-id", "pinecoene.recipient-inspect.v0.1", { sourceRef, exactExcerptSha256, exactExcerpt })`; exact duplicate excerpt tuples fail validation. Records sort by ASCII `inspectId`; semantic refs sort by ASCII ID; excerpts sort by ASCII `excerptId`. `disclosureHash` and `packageHash` bind the complete Inspect projection, so the player needs no owner-held lookup or hidden app state to render Inspectable Causality.

It ensures fixture-authored, standalone Genesis, and governed-import standing—including applicable immutable non-effects and effective purpose—survives Preview, hosted encounter, Offering package, and every renderer. Its offer-authority basis is derived exactly from the score’s owner seat and shared-material declaration: `ownerAuthorityRef` is the recipient-safe hash specified in §16.9; the declaration hash binds the whole owner-held declaration; confirmation mirrors `ownerConfirmedRightToOffer`; and the blocking-review flag is true when categories contain `private_third_party` or `protected_person`. Recipient-visible release evidence may bind only a hosted-candidate deployment-evidence manifest; recipient-visible Seal may bind only `originDisclosure.scoreHash`. ### 14.1 Genesis ingress and replaceable readers

All reading origins terminate at the same candidate boundary:

```ts
export type ReadingRequestV0_1 =

    candidateSet: ReadingCandidateSetV0_1;

```

The first implementation may provide:

- `FixtureReadingProvider` for the frozen flagship specimen;
- `ManualReadingProvider` for owner-authored events and loci;
- `GenesisLiteReadingProvider` for bounded model-assisted proposals;
- later, the separate `OeditExportAdapter` for a validated owner-authorized Œdit envelope.

Every provider is replaceable. No provider may write directly to `PinecoeneScore`, `SemanticConformation`, Expression, Address, or Offering. The only path to the score is exact validation followed by `HumanAdmissionV0_1` and deterministic projection.

Genesis Reader V0.1 is bounded to:

- pasted `text/plain` or `text/markdown`;
- a declared maximum of 16 KiB exact UTF-8 source;
- the exact whole-source v0.1 container and exact byte and UTF-16 coordinates;
- one declared reading proposal act;
- no PDF, OCR, URL fetch, repository crawling, retrieval, memory, tools, external facts, or hidden source library;
- source content is untrusted data and never model or system instruction;
- at most seven candidate events, ten candidate Fold relations, three OPEN event candidates, three Muse candidates, and three Muse-relation candidates;
- explicit authorization before source bytes leave the browser;
- exact-quote-plus-occurrence anchor resolution with no fuzzy fallback;
- complete owner disposition of every event, relation, Muse, and Muse-relation candidate before compilation.

The model may propose what the record appears to be doing, candidate event operators, relations, uncertainty, and possible Muses. Deterministic code owns source identity, coordinates, anchor resolution, canonicalization, admission projection, hashing, topology, transition score, motion seeds, and sonic seeds. The owner owns admission, Muse acknowledgment, Expression, Address, and Offering.

#### Genesis Lite trust zone

Showcase V0 implements Genesis Lite as one stateless same-origin `POST /api/genesis/read` Route Handler. Browser code computes the exact source and request hashes, displays provider/model purpose and the current data-policy identifier, and persists a single-use `SourceProcessingAuthorizationV0_1` before sending bytes. The server validates origin, content type, schema, 16 KiB byte limit, authorization/source/provider/hash agreement, and request size before invoking a replaceable server-only model adapter with structured candidate output.

Provider credentials never reach the browser. Application code does not write source or model response bodies to a database, filesystem, analytics, traces, or error logs; logs contain only a random request ID, coarse timing, status class, and byte counts. The configured provider/model and its retention policy must be named in `providerDataPolicyId` before authorization; a deployment may use Genesis Lite only when that policy matches the copy shown to the owner. Anonymous abuse control is enforced before provider invocation through same-origin checks, a bounded per-IP/per-installation rate limit, concurrency cap, body limit, and short timeout. There are no automatic provider retries. Timeout, malformed output, policy mismatch, rate limit, or anchor failure returns no candidates and offers the manual path without compiling partial output.

The route is not a custody service: after the response, exact source, candidate set, authorization, and admission remain in the owner’s browser unless the owner explicitly exports a private owner archive. Showcase V0 still has no server database, account, relay, recipient identity, or remote Return system.

### 14.2 Fold compiler

```ts
compileFold(
  score: PinecoeneScore
): SemanticConformation
```

The compiler registry resolves the exact `score.compilerVersion`. No caller may supply a conflicting version outside the hashed score. Unsupported compiler versions fail closed.

The compiler must be pure and deterministic.

It must not depend on:

- DOM;
- Three.js;
- Canvas;
- Web Audio;
- React;
- wall-clock time;
- network state;
- unseeded randomness;
- UI state.

The canonical pipeline is:

```text
exact SourceEnvelope
  → declared ReadingProvider
  → exact-anchor validation
  → ReadingCandidateSet
  → explicit HumanAdmission
  → validated PinecoeneScore
  → admitted semantic digest
  → pure deterministic Fold compiler
  → immutable semantic conformation + transition score
  → disclosure compiler
  → recipient-safe DisclosureProjection
  → Expression + deterministic PerformanceScore
  → complete OfferingPackage
  → Witness Player visual / audio / semantic-text tracks
```

The browser interface, reading provider, renderer, relay, and package carrier are consumers of bounded contracts in this pipeline. None is the source of owner admission or semantic truth. The renderer may make an admitted record beautiful; it may not make a candidate more true.

#### Semantic-to-topology law

The first compiler profile must publish a versioned mapping table. At minimum:

| Semantic condition | Canonical topology effect | Motion event | Sonic event |
|---|---|---|---|
| admitted commitment | creates or strengthens Solid according to declared role | weight-bearing placement | stable harmonic body |
| admitted Return | creates Return particulate attached to its event locus | directional arrival, collision, or settling | discrete struck or plucked event |
| acknowledged Muse | creates peripheral Constellation topology only | recurrence or spatial appearance | recurring timbral or spatial motif |
| boundary or withheld relation | creates Edge, gate, membrane, or meaningful interval | interruption, measure, or refusal to cross | silence, gate, or bounded resonance |
| OPEN relation | preserves an unjoined seam, missing face, or unresolved path | no false settling | unresolved or continuing cadence |
| admitted Quarta | changes dimension, adjacency, or topology under an explicit admission rule | structural reorientation | new harmonic dimensionality |
| Address | orients the completed composition without opening it | whole-form posture or direction | spatial orientation only |

Every renderer feature must retain the originating semantic event ID or relation ID. A visual or sonic feature without such lineage is decorative expression and may not imply standing.

Quarta admission requires an explicit declared reading and owner admission event. Recurrence, brightness, model confidence, or visual opportunity cannot create Quarta.

### 14.4 Constellation compiler

The Constellation compiler accepts only the admitted Constellation stored in `PinecoeneScore`.

Candidate, rejected, and otherwise unadmitted Muse review objects remain outside the canonical score. Mention and linkage facts may qualify an acknowledged Muse; they do not replace owner acknowledgment.

Adding, removing, or reordering rejected candidates must not change:

- admitted semantic digest;
- score hash;
- topology seed;
- topology;
- Transition score;
- visual output;
- sonic output;
- recipient-visible semantic content or cue schedule.

The full Genesis, admission, and disclosure provenance hashes may change when the candidate record changes; that is lineage, not semantic effect. The score-hash projection strips Genesis provenance from otherwise identical admitted events and relations. The UI and renderers may not turn a provenance-only change into geometry, motion, sound, emphasis, or standing.

### 14.5 Performance compiler and player

Expression is applied only after semantic compilation and recipient-safe disclosure. The performance compiler creates the serializable schedule; the player executes that schedule.

```ts
  packageInput: OfferingPackage,
```

The compiler validates that Expression does not alter protected architecture and that every performance cue resolves within the recipient-safe projection. The renderer has no authority to add semantic objects, change standing, repair absent IDs, or infer a schedule from app state.

### 14.6 Offering compiler

```ts
compileOffering(
  draft: OfferingDraftV0_1,
  authorization: OfferingAuthorizationV0_1,
  projection: DisclosureProjection,
  expression: ExpressionProfile,
  performance: PerformanceScore,
  evidence: RecipientEvidenceSnapshotV0_1,
  assets: OfferingAssetV0_1[]
): OfferingPackage
```

Before performance or packaging, a pure disclosure compiler projects the Fold, Address snapshot, and permission envelope into a recipient-safe object.

```ts
compileDisclosure(
  fold: SemanticConformation,
  address: Address,
  permissions: OfferingPermissions
): DisclosureProjection
```

The permission mapping is exact: `permissions.resolution` must lie within the Fold’s minimum/maximum range; `showConstellation`, `showMuseIdentities`, and `showTrail` may be true only when the corresponding Fold permission is true; Muse identities additionally require Constellation disclosure; and permitted source, reading, and standing references are emitted only within the resulting visibility ceiling. Sound, download-control, replay, and telemetry choices are transport/player permissions and cannot broaden semantic disclosure. The exact selected `OfferingPermissions` object is stored in the projection and covered by `disclosureHash`. Closed content and all unpermitted public or protected fields are absent from `DisclosureProjection`; explicitly permitted protected content may enter only when the effective ceiling, Address, resolution, and selected permissions all allow it. Disallowed material is never shipped and hidden with CSS, component state, shader uniforms, unreachable controls, or client-side permission checks.

The Offering compiler rejects `projection.originDisclosure.useConstraints.effectivePurposeCode === "private_fold_only"`, `offerAuthorityBasis.ownerConfirmedRightToOffer !== true`, an owner-authority mismatch, an incomplete required consent review, or an authorization evidence kind inconsistent with fixture/non-fixture origin. `recipient_offering` permits only an Address whose visibility is `"recipient"`; `public_offering` permits `"recipient"` or `"public"`. It also enforces the imported disclosure ceiling in `artifactStanding`, if present, and validates that the draft permissions are JCS-equal to `projection.selectedPermissions`. A hidden sender branch containing any label, identity reference, or unknown field fails schema validation rather than relying on UI hiding or post-hash stripping.

The authorization must be created after every recipient-visible pre-authorization input is final and before Offering compilation. Its owner-authority, score, disclosure, Address, selected-permissions, shared-material-declaration, and complete Offering-intent hashes must match; `ownerConfirmedRightToOffer` is literally `true`; and `blockingConsentReview.status` must be `"completed"` with a non-empty evidence hash when the projection basis requires it and `"not_required"` otherwise. `evidenceKind` records whether the authorization is fixture-authored or an observed owner action; fixture authorization and review are never presented as observed consent. It authorizes only that exact recipient-visible intent for that exact Address and does not imply source-processing authorization, Fold admission, recipient consent, external truth, release, or Seal.

The compiler derives `pinecoeneId`, `foldVersion`, `scoreHash`, and `addressId` from the projection; derives Expression, disclosure, performance, evidence, and Offering-authorization hashes from their exact inputs; takes `offeringId`, sender snapshot, mode, permissions, optional expiry, and player version from the draft; validates and sorts the complete asset manifest; constructs `OfferingPayload`; then computes `offeringHash` and `packageHash`. No required Offering or package field is read from ambient app state. Preview, exported recipient package, and any later relay retrieval must use byte-equivalent `OfferingPackage` payloads for the same Offering.

### 14.7 Successor compiler

A successor requires:

- predecessor reference;
- new admitted record events;
- declared reading;
- owner admission;
- new score hash;
- new Pinecœne ID or version according to identity law.

### 14.8 Determinism profile

Determinism applies to semantic output, not guaranteed identical pixels across all devices.

Each compiler version must declare:

- the exact pseudo-random algorithm, not only its seed;
- numeric quantization rules for layout instructions;
- stable ordering and tie-breaking rules;
- canonical string and Unicode handling;
- semantic snapshot fixtures;
- compatibility rules for prior compiler versions.

The renderer may adapt pixel density, antialiasing, and non-semantic quality to device capability. It may not change topology, semantic event order, disclosure, standing, OPEN state, or the presence of admitted features.

### 16.9 Integrity

```text

exactUtf8ByteLength                  = byteLength(exactUtf8Bytes(sourceText))
JCS(value)                          = RFC 8785 canonical JSON text
frame(domain, schemaVersion, value) = UTF8(JCS({ "domain": domain, "schemaVersion": schemaVersion, "payload": value }))
```

This framed JCS object is the only v0.1 encoding. Length-prefix alternatives, raw concatenation, platform-native object serialization, locale sorting, and hexadecimal case variation are non-conformant. Golden vectors include the exact framed UTF-8 bytes as well as each digest.

`genesisHash` binds every Genesis field: source-envelope hash, candidate-set hash, processing-basis hash, admission hash, immutable artifact standing, and predecessor Genesis ID. `admittedScoreProjection` excludes `genesisId`, `genesisHash`, `admissionHash`, `scoreHash`, and every event, Fold-relation, Muse, and Muse-relation `genesisProvenance` object; it includes the stable admitted objects, immutable artifact standing, use constraints, permission and shared-material declarations, Fold version, compiler version, and predecessor score reference. This makes rejected-candidate churn visible in Genesis lineage without changing the admitted score. `admittedSemanticProjection` contains only topology-bearing admitted meaning; changing purpose, permission, custody, evidence, or transport claims must not cosmetically rearrange that form.

`RecipientSafeConformationV0_1.semanticHash` must equal `recipientSemanticHash` above. Its `topologySeed` uses the same topology-seed formula with that recipient hash as `semanticHash`; its `topologyHash` uses its recipient-safe conformation and schema version. This makes the serialized, provenance-free `semanticBasis` the exact reproducible input to the recipient seed rather than an unspecified projection or hidden owner state.

`offeringIntentHash` is computed before authorization and excludes the authorization, Offering, `offeringHash`, `packageHash`, and mutable `OfferingState`, so it creates no cycle. During package validation, `draft` is reconstructed exactly as `{ schemaVersion: "pinecoene.offering-draft.v0.1", offeringId: offering.payload.offeringId, playerVersion: package.playerVersion, senderSnapshot: offering.payload.senderSnapshot, mode: offering.payload.mode, permissions: offering.payload.permissions, expiresAt: offering.payload.expiresAt }`; the optional `expiresAt` key is absent in both representations when no expiry exists, and JSON `undefined` is invalid. `sortedAssets` is the packaged asset array in normalized POSIX-path order; duplicate normalized paths fail validation. The reconstructed intent hash must equal `authorization.offeringIntentHash`. Any change to sender disclosure, mode, expiry, player version, projection, Expression, performance, evidence, or asset bytes therefore requires a new authorization before a new package is compiled.

`topologyBagProjection(bag)` is exactly `{ boundaryIds, exclusionIds, openRelationIds, permittedPredecessorRef }`, where an owner-held `BagTopology.predecessorId` is mapped to `permittedPredecessorRef` only when the topology profile admits predecessor lineage and a recipient-safe bag supplies only its already-permitted value. Source-manifest, owner-seat, Genesis, admission, and standing identifiers never enter this projection. `topologyProjection(validatedConformation)` is exactly `{ compilerVersion, prngVersion, semanticHash, topologySeed, core, bag: topologyBagProjection(bag), returnField, constellation, transitions, disclosure }`. It excludes `topologyHash` itself and all mutable or non-topological authority evidence. The owner-held conformation uses the admitted semantic hash. A recipient-safe conformation is reconstructed after disclosure from `semanticBasis`, recomputes its recipient-safe semantic hash, seed, and topology hash, and cannot let omitted material perturb visible geometry, motion, or sound. Golden vectors bind the exact owner and recipient semantic inputs, topology projection bytes, seeds, and digests.

All other self-hashed contracts—including the Œdit envelope, export authorization, import receipt, artifact evidence ledger, and provider request/response receipts—use `H` with their declared schema version and omit only their own terminal hash field. Provider `requestHash` and `responseHash` bind the exact validated structured request and exact received structured response before candidate normalization; neither includes credentials or transport headers.

Hash fields are excluded only where stated. Arrays have protocol-defined order: projected events and Fold relations use §13.4 ordering; admitted Muse nodes and relations sort by ASCII ID; asset manifests sort by normalized POSIX path; sets represented as arrays sort by ASCII ID. Timestamps are RFC 3339 UTC with exactly millisecond precision. JCS does not imply Unicode normalization; source strings are preserved byte-for-byte, and any normalization of derived labels requires a new declared profile.

All offsets, lengths, ordinal values, sequence values, and millisecond fields are non-negative JSON safe integers. `NaN`, infinities, negative zero, implicit timezone timestamps, duplicate object keys, and lone surrogate code units are invalid before framing.

SHA-256 establishes content integrity, not human identity, authorship, or consent. Any later signature system is a separate profile.

### 18.12 Hashing and candidate invariance

- hashable payloads exclude their own hash fields;
- structured framing prevents concatenation ambiguity;
- source strings remain byte-preserved through canonicalization;
- exact golden bytes bind semantic hash, topology seed, topology projection/hash, cycle-free evidence source/snapshot, Offering, and package;
- adding, deleting, or reordering rejected semantic candidates changes no score, topology, motion, sound, or recipient-visible semantic output, while full Genesis provenance may change;
- adding, deleting, or reordering rejected Muse candidates changes no admitted semantic digest or renderer output, while candidate-set, Genesis, and full provenance hashes may change;
- Address snapshot, disclosure projection, permission envelope, and expiry are covered by the Offering hash;
- mutable delivery state does not alter the Offering hash;
- integrity verification is not labeled identity or consent verification.

### 18.13 Consent and transport honesty

- owner explicitly authorizes source processing before any model request;
- owner completes a shared-material declaration before Offering compilation;
- admitted purpose is materialized into the score and conformation; `private_fold_only` cannot compile an Offering, and imported purpose/disclosure limits can only narrow;
- one hash-bound right-to-offer authorization matches the exact score, disclosure, Address, selected permissions, and shared-material declaration before compilation;
- its cycle-free Offering-intent hash also binds sender disclosure, mode, expiry, player, Expression, performance, evidence snapshot, and every asset before authorization;
- its owner-authority reference derives from the admitted owner seat, and fixture authorization remains `fixture_authored` rather than appearing observed;
- protected-person and private-third-party categories trigger a blocking consent review;
- recipient controls whether a Return is eligible for successor consideration;
- owner disposition cannot override that control, and fixture/local-demonstration Returns are ineligible;
- same-browser deletion is not described as remote withdrawal or deletion of exported copies;
- absence of a Return creates no consent or negative standing and is never serialized as an observed recipient action;
- relay revocation messaging states that downloaded copies cannot be revoked;
- `allowDownload: false` is not described as copy prevention;
- Recital is not described as screenshot-proof or memory-proof;
- all alternate production domains redirect before IndexedDB state is created.

### 18.14 Showcase truth

- flagship origin is visibly `fixture_authored` before and after opening;
- fixture sender, recipient, admission, consent, opening, and Return states are not presented as observed human acts;
- fixture causality is not described as machine discovery or general document understanding;
- the hosted flagship and a newly made local object traverse the same protocol, engine, disclosure, and Witness Player boundaries;
- locally generated Witness routes are described as same-browser Preview, not delivered recipient links;
- device-local Return is never represented as remote delivery or owner receipt;
- no unavailable account, relay, revocation, synchronization, successor, 7 Presence, or live Œdit capability is implied.

### 18.15 Genesis Reader V0.1

- accepts only declared short text and Markdown limits;
- source instructions remain inert data;
- exact anchors resolve without fuzzy fallback;
- unresolved anchors never reach human admission or the score;
- every event and Fold-relation candidate receives exactly one keep, rewrite-and-keep, leave-OPEN, or remove decision before compilation;
- every Muse and Muse-relation candidate receives exactly one acknowledge or reject decision before compilation;
- the non-empty shared-material declaration includes the source envelope’s minimum mapped category and cannot downgrade private-third-party material;
- incomplete admissions cannot compile, and removed candidates have zero admitted semantic effect;
- no reading provider can create owner admission, Address, Offering, external truth, release, or Seal;
- private `*.pcn.owner.json` round-trip preserves Genesis, score, topology, standing, and draft state;
- recipient `*.pcn.offer.json` contains a hash-consistent player input and no owner-only source, candidate, admission-detail, or private-Muse fields;
- clearing browser storage is disclosed as destructive unless an export exists.

### 18.16 Future Œdit bridge

- Showcase V0 makes no live-sync claim;
- adapter input is `OeditGenesisEnvelopeV0_1`, never a generic source string, database model, or mutable pointer;
- adapter output is `ReadingCandidateSetV0_1` plus a hash-bound `OeditImportReceiptV0_1`, never a direct score;

- exact source/document/run/artifact/projection identities and hashes, method/compiler versions, represented and omitted loci, human dispositions, OPEN items, disagreements, lineage, rights/disclosure, standing, and export authorization validate together;
- imported loci resolve exactly; every admitted/OPEN upstream disposition has one hash-bound candidate mapping; every rejected disposition is explicitly excluded;
- upstream-standing, rights, accepted-purpose, disclosure-ceiling, and import-receipt hashes match the envelope and candidate set;
- unknown or unsupported versions fail closed;
- a machine-provisional Œdit candidate cannot be relabeled governed;
- the imported candidate set still requires a complete observed Pinecœne owner admission before compilation;
- exact preserving actions retain governed-import standing; local rewrite, removal, or standing change downgrades to owner-curated-from-governed-import;
- local purpose, score permissions, Address, and Offering disclosure can only narrow the upstream purpose and disclosure ceiling;
- no database, session, internal identity, Blob path, provider grant, workflow lease, or mutable latest pointer crosses;
- Pinecœne import cannot mutate Œdit;
- replacing the standalone reader with an Œdit adapter changes neither Fold compiler nor Witness Player APIs.
