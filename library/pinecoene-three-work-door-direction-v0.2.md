*One door. One living protagonist. Three works in lineage.*  
*LKN-PCN-DOOR-DIRECTION-002 · 27 August 2026*

## 1. The clarification

Pinecœne currently has three works. They are not alternative demos and should not compete as three hero objects. They form a lineage.

### 1.1 Genesis

The first source folded into form.

Its role is to prove that an admitted causal record can earn particular geometry. It is the ancestor: settled, inspectable and dark at rest.

### 1.2 The Genesis Chat

The conversation in which Pinecœne first took form.

`This Chat` is an indexical working label and should disappear from the public experience. `PCN-0002` may remain its internal identity. The working public name is **The Genesis Chat**; a more literary presentation may use **The conversation that made Pinecœne**.

This work is not a generic chat visualization. Its record contains the actual discoveries and corrections that produced Pinecœne: the first schematics, the beautiful lie, the object, prior-art review, living state and the unresolved merge.

### 1.3 Pinecœne, Becoming

The present site being made again after its earlier translations failed.

This is the living V3 work and the protagonist of the Door. `V3` belongs in its receipt and lineage, not across its public face. Until its record is curated and compiled, it must be described as a current becoming or living study—not as a settled canonical Fold.

Working public title: **Pinecœne, Becoming**  
Working receipt: `third attempt · current · OPEN`

The earlier failures are not embarrassing backstory to conceal. They are causal events that changed the current work. Showing those corrections is exactly what a Pinecœne should be able to do.

## 2. One Door, not three entrances

The homepage presents one object: **Pinecœne, Becoming**.

Genesis and The Genesis Chat do not appear beside it as equal cards, a carousel or a specimen picker. They enter during the present work’s Becoming at the moments when they actually affect it. They may appear as ancestor forms or dock into its lineage, but they never morph into, merge with or impersonate the current Fold.

The public sequence is:

1. Encounter the current Fold.
2. Read the letter while the object remains present.
3. Watch this Fold become from its record.
4. Visit Works to encounter all three individually.
5. Receive an honest invitation to participate.

The `/works` shelf then presents the three chronologically:

1. Genesis.
2. The Genesis Chat.
3. Pinecœne, Becoming — current and OPEN.

The Thin Fold is no longer the owed third work. It moves to `More → What’s next` as a future test.

## 3. What the prototypes already discovered

The prototypes contain much of the real medium. Their failure was chiefly presentation: the excellent object grammar was buried inside an expert instrument.

### 3.1 Fold and Becoming are two projections of one work

The Fold is the work at rest. The Becoming is its causal record replayed.

The Door begins with the Fold already present. A first visitor must not watch an empty field for a long time before being allowed to see the thing. `Watch one become` restarts the same object from its first admitted event and plays toward the form already encountered.

### 3.2 The object is handled

Recover the prototype’s real physical encounter:

- drag or pointer movement turns the Fold;
- scroll or an equivalent control approaches it;
- a very slow witness drift may reveal dimensionality before interaction;
- the automatic drift stops after the visitor takes hold;
- the Fold eventually settles rather than performing forever;
- reduced motion presents a lawful still state and explicit causal steps.

The visitor should be able to discover an unresolved seam by circling the object before the interface explains OPEN.

### 3.3 Motion is semantic

The strongest prototype law is:

> The Fold remains still enough to be encountered; only the record earns movement.

Useful causal vocabulary already present in the prototypes:

- a proposal opens space;
- a Return traces, stamps or condenses at its event locus;
- evidence creates a blue field with a source;
- evaluation hardens an earned edge;
- whole-admission creates committed volume exactly once;
- a boundary creates a membrane, edge, interval or gate;
- Address turns the whole form toward something without changing its topology;
- correction visibly changes the work’s structure;
- OPEN halts a path, face or closing edge rather than decorating absence;
- a meaningful landing receives a brief reflection, then the light leaves;
- the replay ends at rest, not in perpetual breathing.

No particle, glow, pulse or sound exists merely to suggest intelligence or life.

### 3.4 The Locket contains the right dramaturgy

The Locket is not the Pinecœne itself. It is the recipient-facing permission vessel. Its pale radial form is not the universal visual language, but its encounter sequence is excellent:

1. A quiet arrival with the closed object centered.
2. One human line.
3. A single affirmative action, with Not now and Decline remaining possible.
4. Opening in the order the work was made.
5. One caption per visible consequence.
6. The kept gap: not painted over.
7. One unresolved seam.
8. Complete stillness after opening.
9. Optional touch-to-understand only after the encounter.
10. A Return later, framed as “a line is enough.”

This choreography can inform the public Door without turning the Door into an Offering or simulating recipient custody.

### 3.5 Correction belongs inside the work

The prototype’s phrase **the beautiful lie corrected** should guide V3.

The first object once gained volume at every evaluation because the rhythm looked elegant. The record did not authorize it. Correcting that mistake became part of the Lineage. The same honesty should govern the site: each failed translation is an event, not an embarrassment removed from view.

### 3.6 Mark → Form → Inspect

The existing resolution idea provides progressive disclosure without an explanatory homepage:

- **Mark:** the smallest sign of the work; useful for loading and the navigation pulse.
- **Form:** the object encountered and turned at the Door.
- **Inspect:** causal replay, anatomy, citations and deeper Studio material.

The Door begins at Form. It does not begin at Inspect.

## 4. The current V3 record

The present Fold should be compiled from a curated, sanitized record of Pinecœne becoming a public experience. A provisional seven-phase reading is:

### Phase 01 — The portable thought

Pinecœne emerges from Œdit: perhaps an idea can travel as a form that retains how it came to be.

### Phase 02 — Genesis

Genesis proves that a record can produce particular, earned geometry rather than an arbitrary visualization.

### Phase 03 — The Genesis Chat

The conversation discovers Fold and Becoming, semantic motion, OPEN, Lineage, the living state and Offering.

### Phase 04 — The vessel mistaken for the work

The first public translation foregrounds the Locket. The recipient ritual is beautiful, but the vessel is mistaken for the product.

### Phase 05 — The machinery recovered

Studio and the executable Fold return. The deterministic system becomes demonstrable, but the public arrives inside an expert instrument rather than meeting the object.

### Phase 06 — The explanation mistaken for the door

The next public site tries to explain Pinecœne before allowing anyone to encounter one. The constitution, taxonomy and qualifications bury the work they are meant to protect.

### Phase 07 — Object first

Deniz intervenes: one door, object first, the letter as the walk, participation as the last beat. The present work remains OPEN because the site and the medium are still being cared into form.

This reading is provisional. The final prototype should help determine the exact events, their semantic roles and the geometry they earn.

## 5. The essential first experience

### Beat 0 — the thing

Full dark. The V3 Fold is already present and slowly turning. Only the Pinecœne wordmark is visible.

After the visitor has seen it, or after a short pause:

> This is a pinecone.

Then:

> Turn it.

No status badges, hashes, taxonomy, candidate labels or explanatory essay occupy this moment.

### Beats 1–4 — the letter

The existing Door letter remains the narrative spine:

- ideas are scary;
- ideas arrive ugly, fragile and threatening to the known;
- care can make them beautiful;
- courage is required to enter the world an idea came from;
- a thought may need a shape to travel;
- the object already encountered is one of those shapes.

The Fold remains present throughout. The writing and spatial composition change around it; the object does not disappear so that the site can lecture about it.

### Beat 5 — the invitation

The homepage ends with three actions:

#### Watch one become

Replay the current V3 Fold’s own record. Each source beat produces one legible consequence. Genesis and The Genesis Chat appear only where they causally enter the record. The final state returns to the same OPEN object encountered at the beginning.

#### See the works

Enter the chronological three-work lineage.

#### Bring an idea

For now this is a truthful placeholder, not an intake simulation.

Suggested boundary:

> The invitation is real. The receiving door is not open yet.

The destination may offer the actions that genuinely exist now:

- watch a becoming;
- fork a local sketch;
- learn what bringing a fuller record will eventually involve.

`Offer one unfinished sentence` may be shown as coming later. Until real custody and feedback exist, there should be no textarea, fake submission, delivery confirmation or implication that a human received anything.

## 6. Navigation as a pulse

Navigation is a small pulse where a visitor expects a menu: top right on desktop and mobile.

It should feel like the object’s Mark, but function as an ordinary, dependable menu button:

- at least a 44 × 44 pixel interaction target;
- an accessible name;
- visible keyboard focus;
- static under reduced motion;
- one or two quiet breaths before settling, not endless attention-seeking;
- no mystery about whether it can be operated.

It opens only:

- Works;
- Join;
- More.

`More` contains Science, Art, How it’s made and What’s next. These remain latches for curiosity and never walk in front of the Pinecœne.

## 7. Material and presentation direction

Recover:

- the Genesis Fold’s crystalline, source-specific anatomy;
- nested Core, evidence fields, boundaries and permanently unresolved topology;
- dark material at rest;
- warm committed reflection and evidence blue;
- transient light rather than ambient glow;
- causal replay;
- phase docking and lineage;
- meaningful stillness;
- inspectable incompletion;
- the Locket’s consent-led opening rhythm;
- editorial Lineage language such as “How we ended up here.”

Do not recover:

- translucent AI-orb membranes;
- decorative particle fog;
- unconditional breathing or central glow;
- a generic pale rosette as the universal Fold;
- telemetry, hashes, resolution codes or owner controls at first encounter;
- the six-step journey rail;
- three equal hero cards or a conventional carousel;
- long mandatory playback;
- pointer-only anatomy;
- owner and recipient controls on one surface;
- any fake feedback, delivery or custody loop.

The carrier must become quiet enough for the cargo to be encountered.

## 8. Honest standing

The experience may be visually complete while remaining truthful about its current machinery:

- Genesis and The Genesis Chat remain curated, fixture-authored studies unless their standing changes through explicit human review.
- Pinecœne, Becoming is a curated living record of this site’s development, not an arbitrary-source AI reading.
- The Door demonstrates real deterministic form and replay without claiming accounts, remote custody, delivery, acceptance or live Œdit synchronization.
- Browser-local forks remain labelled as studies and never impersonate their ancestors.
- Locket and Return mechanisms shown publicly must not imply real-world transport until such transport exists.

These disclosures belong at the relevant threshold and in Inspect—not over the first object.

The updated prototype should help settle:

2. Whether **Pinecœne, Becoming** is the right title for the V3 work, or whether it should remain publicly unnamed.
3. The exact material identity and silhouette of the V3 Fold.
4. How Genesis and The Genesis Chat enter its Becoming without becoming explanatory thumbnails.
5. Which failures and corrections belong in the public replay and which remain in deeper Lineage.
6. How the letter and object share the viewport across desktop and mobile.
7. The minimum visible language needed to make turning, replay and OPEN discoverable.
8. The exact placeholder treatment for Bring an idea.
9. Whether the navigation pulse is derived directly from the V3 Mark.
10. The final transition from the invitation into Works, Join and the deeper instrument.

## 10. Test of the direction

A first visitor should be able to answer, without reading technical documentation:

1. **What is that?**  
   A pinecone: a thought given a form that remembers how it came to be.

2. **Why might someone need one?**  
   Some ideas need a shape to travel without being falsely cleaned up or closed.

3. **What happened here?**  
   The object and site were made, misunderstood, corrected and are still becoming.

4. **What can I do?**  
   Turn one, watch one become, see the works and approach the unfinished invitation to help make one.

If the visitor meets an instrument, manifesto or permission vessel before meeting the Fold, the Door has failed again.
