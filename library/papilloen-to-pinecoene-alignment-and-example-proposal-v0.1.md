## 1. Executive conclusion

Papillœn should become an example inside Pinecœne.

The exact relationship matters:

> **Papillœn remains its own candidate research instrument. Pinecœne may create a distinct, traceable Fold from an explicitly admitted projection of Papillœn's record.**

The public example must not imply that Papillœn itself is a Pinecœne, that Pinecœne authored Papillœn, or that converting it into a Fold changes Papillœn's standing. It should show what Pinecœne does to a real, bounded, intellectually serious source:

1. receives the source without silently interpreting it;
2. separates assertion, theorem, authored reading, evidence, refusal, uncertainty, and missing authority;
3. lets an owner admit or reject each candidate;
4. compiles only admitted meaning into form;
5. preserves Papillœn's `CANDIDATE · UNSEALED` standing and OPEN questions;
6. exposes the trace from source to every visible consequence;
7. produces an object that can travel without impersonating its source.

This would be the best proof yet that Pinecœne is not a decorative visualizer. Genesis demonstrates the medium from an internal founding record. A Papillœn study would demonstrate that the medium can receive another work while preserving its sovereignty, epistemic types, refusals, and incompleteness.

Papillœn also changes how Pinecœne itself should be rebuilt. It supplies a constitutional discipline rather than a visual theme:

> **Geometry is a typed claim. Materiality is earned. Motion testifies. Admission leaves a trace. Standing stays visible.**

## 3. What Papillœn is

Papillœn is a single, clinically precise proof object for the declared structure of Box 7. It is not a marketing website, cinematic essay, decorative network, generic system map, or autonomous agent display.

Its governing proposition is embodied as two regular tetrahedra sharing exactly one vertex:

| Seat | Instrument | Verb | Wing |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cosmic Library | remembers | record / field |
| 2 | GetReceipts | witnesses | record / field |
| 3 | PromiseMe | binds | record / field |
| 4 | Lakin | admits | shared point / gateway |
| 5 | Lœgos | operates | work / form |
| 6 | Œdit | examines* | work / form |
| 7 | Pinecœne | seeds | work / form |

The declared tetrahedra are:

```text
T− = {1, 2, 3, 4}
T+ = {4, 5, 6, 7}
```

The object has seven vertices and twelve structural edges. Seat 4 is the unique cut vertex: removing it separates the two wings, while removing any other single seat leaves the graph connected.

Its central law is:

> **Every lawful standing-bearing cross-half passage must cross seat 4 and leave a trace.**

Seat 4 is a logical and constitutional admission gateway. Papillœn does not claim that every network request or software call must physically route through a single Lakin service.

The process axis runs from field to form. The declared involution

```text
σ = (1 7)(2 6)(3 5)(4)
```

expresses correspondence across the wings. It is visibly typed as a declared relation, not misrepresented as a structural edge, direct route, or proved theorem.

The asterisk beside Œdit's `examines*` is material. The repository treats revision ownership as open or contested rather than silently assigning final authority to Œdit, a reader, or a model.

## 4. How the executable Papillœn works

Papillœn implements its argument as an inspectable instrument rather than a sequence of explanatory pages.

### 4.1 Exact object

The seven coordinates and twelve edges are frozen. Projection is deterministic and renderer-light: a hand-rolled orthographic projection produces an edge-only SVG. The instrument does not depend on Three.js or WebGL.

Faces and volume are withheld because the candidate standing has not earned volumetric merger. The refusal is semantic, not a missing visual effect.

### 4.2 Interactions as demonstrations

- **Orbit** changes observation without changing identity.
- **σ mirror** illuminates declared correspondences through seat 4 without morphing vertices or inventing structural edges.
- **0D aperture** exposes the shared point.
- **1D aperture** exposes a lawful path such as `2 → 4 → 6`.
- **2D aperture** acts as an observation lens, not a shared face.
- **3D request** is refused because merger would collapse distinct interiors and overstate disclosure.
- **œ overlay** contributes an authored operator—closed record wing and open work wing—without pretending it is a theorem of the bare graph.
- **Typing inspector** exposes identity, measurement, epistemic type, standing, and invariant.
- **Meno probe** lets the visitor remove a seat and produce the cut-vertex proof directly.

### 4.3 Conformance as visible evidence

The instrument exposes its invariants and geometry checksum instead of treating testing as backstage implementation detail. A machine-readable `/conformance.json` expresses the same candidate identity.

### 4.4 Visual and motion law

The design is intentionally quiet:

- deep dark field and bone lines;
- no accent palette;
- no faces or ornamental mass;
- no gradients, particles, ambient drift, or decorative energy;
- no random state;
- no idle motion;
- deterministic starting frame;
- motion only for input, state change, or theorem demonstration.

Papillœn's beauty comes from precision. The object remains quiet enough that a one-pixel error feels loud.

### 4.5 Passport and standing

The footer exposes artifact identity, standing, source brief, absent sources, build identity, geometry checksum, and known exceptions. It does not allow polish to imply acceptance or Seal.

## 5. What Papillœn teaches Pinecœne

Papillœn does not ask Pinecœne to become monochrome or edge-only. It asks Pinecœne to become more exact about what its beauty claims.

### 5.1 The Fourth Point changes the source-to-Fold pipeline

Pinecœne should formalize this movement:

```text
received source
    ↓
reader candidates
    ↓
owner admission / rejection / OPEN disposition
    ↓  every accepted crossing leaves a trace
semantic conformation
    ↓
Fold
    ↓
Offering projection
    ↓
recipient Encounter
    ↓
Return candidate
    ↓
owner disposition
```

Source must never become standing-bearing geometry merely because a model extracted a plausible idea. Receipt, candidate generation, admission, recognition, commitment, projection, and publication are different events.

### 5.2 Geometry is a typed claim

Every visible feature in a Pinecœne must answer:

- which admitted source event or relation produced it;
- what semantic class it represents;
- what standing it carries;
- which invariant constrains it;
- whether it is structural, evidentiary, authored, expressive, or experimental;
- whether it is owner-only or recipient-permitted;
- what remains withheld or OPEN.

The renderer is not an illustrator placed after the compiler. It is a projection of typed semantic standing.

### 5.3 Materiality is earned

Pinecœne may remain materially rich, but its material grammar should become stricter:

- admitted commitments may create or strengthen Solid;
- evidence may create evidence-blue events or particulate fields at attributable loci;
- acknowledged Muses may create peripheral Constellation topology;
- boundaries may create edges, membranes, gates, or intervals;
- OPEN relations must preserve gaps, missing faces, or unresolved paths;
- Address may orient the whole form without rewriting topology;
- Expression may change permitted appearance without changing semantic identity;
- rejected, private, and unreviewed material must affect no recipient-visible pixel, motion, sound, accessibility text, package byte, or downloadable artifact.

Volume, glow, and density must never be automatic rewards supplied by the renderer.

### 5.4 Motion testifies

The settled Fold should rest. Movement is lawful when it expresses:

- direct visitor operation;
- an admitted transition during Becoming;
- a disclosure or permission transition;
- the opening of a Locket;
- an Offering or Return state transition;
- an explicitly authored Expression whose standing is disclosed.

Ambient motion that merely suggests life, consciousness, intelligence, or importance should be removed. Completion should end in stillness.

### 5.5 Inspection becomes epistemic

Pinecœne's existing semantic inspector should evolve into a true typing inspector. Spatial selection and textual inspection must be two views of the same renderer-neutral feature record.

Recommended fields include:

```text
feature identity
semantic role
epistemic type
source references
admission decision reference
standing
invariant
projection/disclosure state
recipient visibility
inspection copy
known exception or OPEN condition
```

### 5.6 Conformance becomes part of the artifact

Published Pinecœnes should expose a human-readable passport and a machine-readable conformance report proving, at minimum:

- deterministic semantic, topology, scene, replay, and package identities;
- preservation of OPEN relations;
- absence of rejected/private influence;
- Expression/topology separation;
- Address/orientation separation;
- monotonic recipient disclosure;
- exact source and admission references permitted for that projection;
- build, compiler, fixture, and release identities;
- standing and known exceptions.

### 5.7 Offering and Return are constitutional passages

An Offering is not generic sharing. It is a recipient-safe projection crossing a standing and custody boundary. A Return is not automatic evidence or consent.

Each transition should leave an exact trace of what was projected, withheld, encountered, and returned. A Return becomes only a candidate until the owner separately recognizes, admits, rejects, or leaves it OPEN. Fixture-authored and local-demonstration Returns must not silently generate successors.

### 5.8 Human authority remains explicit

Models and readers may propose readings. Œdit may examine. The deterministic compiler may execute accepted inputs. None of these acts constitutes owner admission, revision, recognition, publication, acceptance, or Seal.

## 6. Why Papillœn is the right Pinecœne example

Papillœn is unusually suitable because its source already distinguishes several kinds of truth that Pinecœne must preserve:

- mathematical properties of the declared graph;
- declared correspondences that are not graph edges;
- authored operators that are not theorems;
- visual refusals that carry meaning;
- executable conformance evidence;
- a contested verb;
- missing formal sources;
- candidate and unsealed standing;
- known boundaries on disclosure and merger.

A weak converter would turn all of this into an impressive symmetrical sculpture and erase the differences. A lawful Pinecœne must do the opposite: make those differences spatially and temporally inspectable.

It also gives the public two genuinely different examples:

| Example | What it proves |
|---|---|
| Genesis | a founding record can become an inspectable, transmissible form |
| Papillœn study | an external work can become a Fold without losing sovereignty, claim type, refusal, provenance, or OPEN standing |

This is more valuable than showing two objects that differ only aesthetically.

### Papillœn is not automatically the Thin Fold

Papillœn is visually thin because it withholds faces and volume, but its source record is not the deliberately weak, weakly challenged record required by the Thin Fold falsification test.

Therefore:

- Papillœn may teach the law that unearned materiality must be withheld;
- it does not satisfy the owed Thin Fold experiment merely because its current renderer is edge-only;
- the Thin Fold should remain visibly owed until its distinct sparse-record test is actually run.

## 9. Pinecœne rebuild direction

The overnight redesign and refactor should align Pinecœne to Papillœn's laws without visually cloning it.

### 9.1 Preserve

- object-first Public Door;
- Genesis as the current real Work;
- deterministic semantic compiler;
- canonical fixture immutability and browser-local study forks;
- Admission and Recognition as owner acts;
- OPEN topology;
- Expression/topology and Address/orientation separation;
- renderer-neutral semantic references;
- Offering package, Locket, Encounter, and bounded Return;
- recipient-safe disclosure and fail-closed acquisition;
- Dexie migration and legacy export boundary;
- reduced-motion and non-WebGL fallbacks;
- the literary, human voice of Pinecœne.

### 9.2 Refactor

#### Standing kernel

Create one explicit standing vocabulary shared by source intake, candidate Reading, Admission, compiler output, public Work, Offering, Return, inspector, passport, and conformance.

#### Admission trace

Make every standing-bearing source-to-form crossing cite an admission decision. The renderer must not accept anonymous or merely inferred semantic material.

#### Typed scene contract

Every renderer-neutral feature should carry semantic references, epistemic type, material role, standing, invariant, inspection copy, projection scope, and disclosure requirements.

#### Earned-material renderer

Start from structural truth. Add material, light, volume, fields, and movement only when admitted standing authorizes them.

#### Rest-first performance

Suspend idle performance. Use motion for causal replay, user operation, disclosure transition, or explicitly typed Expression.

#### Visible inspector and passport

Unify spatial selection, semantic inspection, source trace, conformance, identity, and standing.

#### Machine-readable conformance

Generate exact work and package reports from the same immutable release inputs used by the player.

### 9.3 Rebuild visually

Pinecœne should remain emotionally and materially distinct from Papillœn:

- Papillœn: clinical bone-line instrument, proof through restraint;
- Pinecœne Door: dark human room, literary invitation, object before explanation;
- Pinecœne Studio: precise owner instrument with denser controls and explicit standing;
- Pinecœne Fold: potentially rich material form, but only where meaning has earned it;
- Locket: separate permission vessel and opening dramaturgy;
- Vital Sign: separate experiment, never Fold authority.

The redesign should remove atmospheric effects that do not have semantic standing. It should not remove wonder. Wonder should come from seeing meaning acquire necessary form.

### 9.4 Do not copy

- do not turn all Pinecœne pages into monochrome engineering diagrams;
- do not make every public visitor study Box 7 before encountering a Fold;
- do not replace Pinecœne's rich forms with Papillœn's exact geometry;
- do not reuse Papillœn's candidate object as Pinecœne's topology;
- do not imply Papillœn has been admitted, accepted, published, or sealed merely because this proposal exists;
- do not let a shared suite identity collapse distinct products, sources, owners, or custody boundaries.
